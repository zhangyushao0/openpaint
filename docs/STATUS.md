## Known limits

Limits that a dated line in ROADMAP's Candidates already names, with its way out, are not repeated here.

- Packs: the entry tier is mostly Twemoji; the builder needs the network on a cache miss; `practice-pack search met` fails since the Met retired that search (2026-10-01); a 320-px matte misses thin parts; the user's own pictures' outline is one silhouette; pictures show at most 1,024 px; an import reads the whole file into memory.
- Practice scope: units 6 to 8 empty; kept counts and shares (codes 30 to 34, 49 to 60) read by no model; a lenient eye earns dots as fast as the app's clean; the practice pens' sizes fixed, V大's transition round and textured tip in no kind; only divisions, whole copies, value masses and abstract analysis take a composition.
- Progress (D124): a curve only from what was judged or paired; a pair of any two compared copies, judged or not (S150). An eye whose standard rises under-reads the rise (the pairs recover a part); a random eye reads as a rise where levels rose; a pair's card lays no value mass's or key shift's marks.
- The path: some levels are weak: divisions, whole copies and value masses fall back to medium where a pack holds no hard composition. The review row carries no count.
- The Pomodoro: a test chore's block under way across a restart ends showing the setting's length; a lesson the chain left on another sheet counts as begun, so does one drawn before the block; a How's words checked by reading and by words only the app's workings use (S146).
- Sheets: an exported sheet shows strokes, fills and the pictures under abstract analysis's marks only; the pen chosen, All tools and the overlay's mode not stored; after Next an earlier exercise cannot be undone; a stroke after undoing past the first Compare counts as the copy; a How left with no use of the sheet shows again when it opens; a sheet in portrait shows the pair smaller; the eyedropper over an earlier exercise still remakes it from its generator; a lesson rolled back after a failed write leaves its strokes on the artwork; One more after a test lesson that does not fit leaves for a new day sheet.
- Render passes over whole attachments: a change drawn in place, a stroke's frame with a region over a tile (a big brush, 8x) and the lift's frame still pass over the 1,024-px targets and 2,048-px atlas, and they and the worker's tiles finish with a pass over the whole canvas or tier atlas; a tile's targets and the finishing ones hold up to 11 MiB more.
- Frames: a picture removed from its pack leaves its frames' shape; an upright pair leaves its slot's outer part empty; the faded ground is made on the frame loop (untimed on the tablet).
- Reading the copy: a mark is the last Draw or Correct line within 0.1 of the frame; a shape's fit takes the copy's lines nearest the outline (a copy far off fits its best half); the fit counts an eraser's cut within its radius, pressure not; a colour's numbers take each colour as chosen, not as mixed; white or bare paper reads as bare; the eyedropper compares one pixel.
- Geometry: estimates always in square frames; the solids' vanishing points not shown; an estimate's or a point in space's line: strokes within 7° and a tenth of the longer's length are one edge, a hooked end an edge of its own; free boxes' Compare only runs the horizon on; Construction not stored; a scale of seven cells halves unevenly.
- Divisions and value masses: with the view turned a line's round end may pass the frame by half a pen; a division has no answer; strokes join only end to end (35° at most), a line erased in part counts its pieces; a whole copy's division layer is found by its name; value masses' answers only for pack pictures, the greys fixed (Oklab L 0.35, 0.6, 0.85), a middle rim where light meets dark, masses under 1 % merged; a whole copy's blocks compared by eye and probe only.

- Combinations: a crop's areas are a squint at 128 cells: an area under 5 % of the crop or too thin for a core goes into its neighbour, so a small accent is never read; a crop's recognisable objects are caught only by eye on the sheets; a picture under about 1,400 px on its shorter side gives no crop; a best crop whose answer breaks a rule once packed is dropped, the next best not tried; the scenarios' crops are generated pictures (`test_crops`: two simple, two medium, no hard).

## Working features

- Drawing (D18, D41, D71): only the pen draws, along one Catmull-Rom curve through its samples, pressure, tilt and speed shaping size and opacity, StreamLine smoothing the stored path; undo and redo by two- and three-finger taps and the side bar; leaving mid-stroke commits the stroke; the pen's outline while hovering.
- Brushes (D74): eleven built-ins in four sets plus Recent (Pencil; Ink Pen, Inker, Monoline; Hard Paint, Hard Round, Soft Brush, Textured Round, Flat, Opaque Paint; Speckle); the chosen brush's row tapped opens its editor in place (a live strip, Duplicate, Reset, Delete, its set); sets reorder by holding a row; kept with the toolbox.
- Layers and tools (D46, D50, D51, D66, D70): smudge and eraser on the brush recipes; opacity, four blend modes, Clipping Mask, Alpha Lock, lock, one level of pass-through groups, a mask and a picture each, rows moved by holding, Merge Down; the pen's three side buttons mappable.
- Records (D43, D57, D59, D67, D69): freehand and rectangle selections; Transform (move, scale, turn, Flip, Rotate 45°); Copy, Cut, Copy All, Duplicate, Cut & Paste, Paste (three fingers down, or Actions); QuickShape and Edit shape; the Drawing Guide's four symmetries; Hue/Saturation/Brightness, Curves, Gaussian Blur and Sharpen previewed as kept.
- Fill and colour (D37, D53, D56, D70): ColorDrop with its threshold and Continue filling; HSV wheel, eight recent colours, 24 swatches, hex; the eyedropper's loupe; colour-managed images in and out.
- View, studio, files (D55, D62, D64, D85): pan, pinch-zoom 0.25 to 8 of fit, rotate, a quick pinch fits, the canvas sharp at 2x, 4x or 8x once the fingers stand still, ten bits on the tablet; the home's Studio (cards, search, New canvas: screen, square, A4); OpenPaint files; ten kept versions an artwork; PNG at 1x, 2x, 4x and JPEG up to 2x; Insert image…; a floating reference window; the canvas's way back is Home (首页).
- Look, motion, languages (D40, D44, D97, D100): paper-white line art; panels grow out of their buttons; a frame every vsync while a hand or the interface moves; English or Chinese (简体) chosen in Actions, the system's locale until then, written Chinese-first (V大's words in practice, Procreate's for painting but the author's 压感, 防抖, 色环), a test refusing 13 retired terms.
- Version (D79, D95): "OpenPaint 1.23.3 · format 1" at Actions' end; `dev.openpaint` 1.23.3+format1, version code 12303, debug-signed; a library or file of another format refused untouched.
- Engine (D77, D85, D91 to D93, D96, D97): one render core on the GPU's rasterizer; a stroke from snapshots taken ahead, drawn once a frame (at a view tier into the tier alone, the canvas taking it at its lift, S130); background work while no hand is there; a reopened artwork complete in its first frame (the display cache, written when the canvas is left or the app hidden, never after a save); a lost device made again with a notice; GPU caches within a quarter of the device's memory (2,831 MB on the tablet), let go when hidden; every write one atomic batch; the panel's top rate asked while drawing.
- The home (D116): the front door, Practice's column beside the Studio: the next lesson (Start) or the one under way (Continue), the Pomodoro's row, Review while a redo waits, the unit's skill tiles with three dots, All units, Practice book, Practice options (the packs, Import practice pack…, My pictures, Add my picture…, the colour copies, New copy…).
- The path and lessons (D113 to D115): 8 units after V大's weeks (1 lines, combined lines, colours; 2 shapes, colour combinations, value scales, boxes, free boxes, ellipses; 3 estimates, points in space, solids; 4 objects, divisions, value masses, whole copies; 5 key shifts; 6 to 8 empty); a lesson one skill at one of three levels (入门, 熟练, 稳), 1 to 6 exercises from a new row on the day's sheet; the first lesson a scribble, a straight line and an arc, nothing scored; results Accurate, Almost or Again by eye, or the app's ringed; a lesson's end with dots, seals, One more and That's all for today. ## Known limits

Limits that a dated line in ROADMAP's Candidates already names, with its way out, are not repeated here.

- Packs: the composition pack of format 3 left in the tablet's Download folder (2026-10-30) is refused on import (the library holds its format-4 edition). The entry tier is mostly Twemoji; the builder needs the network on a cache miss; `practice-pack search met` fails since the Met retired that search (2026-10-01); a 320-px matte misses thin parts; the user's own pictures' outline is one silhouette; pictures show at most 1,024 px; an import reads the whole file into memory.
- Practice scope: units 1 to 5 (unit 5 only key shifts), units 6 to 8 empty; no trajectories; kept errors and counts (codes 42 to 58) summed nowhere; the practice pens' sizes fixed, V大's transition round and textured tip in no kind; only divisions, whole copies and value masses take a composition.
- The path: some levels are weak: colour combinations differ in nothing between levels; boxes' levels 2 and 3 alike; lines, combined lines, free boxes and key shifts differ little; divisions, whole copies and value masses fall back to medium where a pack holds no hard composition. An app-judged result shows no reason line; the review row carries no count; dots start at zero for the author's records. Coverage that went with the old scenarios: the long fish shape, the upright division, a busy-ground shape with no outline, a vanishing point inside the original's frame.
- The Pomodoro: a test chore's block under way across a restart ends showing the setting's length; a lesson the chain left on another sheet counts as begun, so does one drawn before the block; a How card's words checked on the screens walked and by reading the tables.
- Sheets: an exported sheet shows strokes and fills only; the pen chosen, All tools and the overlay's mode not stored; after Next an earlier exercise cannot be undone; a stroke after undoing past the first Compare counts as the copy; a How left with no use of the sheet shows again when it opens; a sheet in portrait shows the pair smaller; the eyedropper over an earlier exercise still remakes it from its generator; a lesson rolled back after a failed write leaves its strokes on the artwork; One more after a test lesson that does not fit leaves for a new day sheet; a sheet from before S121c never takes another lesson.
- Frames: a picture removed from its pack leaves its frames' shape; an upright pair leaves its slot's outer part empty; the faded ground is made on the frame loop (untimed on the tablet).
- Reading the copy: a mark is the last Draw or Correct line within 0.1 of the frame; a solid's or object's piece goes to the nearest part within 40 px, so a box drawn with Draw reads as the answer; an ellipse under two thirds round shows as drawn; a shape's fit takes the copy's lines nearest the outline (a copy far off fits its best half); the fit counts an eraser's cut within its radius, pressure not; a colour's numbers take each colour as chosen, not as mixed; white or bare paper reads as bare; the eyedropper compares one pixel.
- Geometry: estimates always in square frames; the solids' vanishing points not shown; strokes within 7° and a tenth of the longer's length are one edge (on simulated free pages 3.5 % of a steady hand's directions said apart; a shaky hand 34 %); a hooked end is an edge of its own; free boxes drawn touching are one; Construction not stored; a 1.14.0 scale of six or seven cells halves unevenly.
- Divisions and value masses: with the view turned a line's round end may pass the frame by half a pen; a division has no answer; strokes join only end to end (35° at most), a line erased in part counts its pieces; a whole copy's division layer is found by its name; value masses' answers only for pack pictures, the greys fixed (Oklab L 0.35, 0.6, 0.85), a middle rim where light meets dark, masses under 1 % merged; a whole copy's blocks compared by eye and probe only.
- Colour table, copies, book: bars scaled within a card; a How card fits in landscape only at four or five short paragraphs; a colour copy keeps no versions; book time before 1.7.0 only between stored times, a crash loses up to a minute, spans in UTC.
- Combinations: a crop's areas are a squint at 128 cells: an area under 5 % of the crop or too thin for a core goes into its neighbour, so a small accent is never read; a crop's recognisable objects are caught only by eye on the sheets; a picture under about 1,400 px on its shorter side gives no crop; a best crop whose answer breaks a rule once packed is dropped, the next best not tried; the How plays on a generated example; the scenarios' crops are generated pictures (`test_crops`).
- Key shifts: a tendency's hue keeps 60° from the crop's main hue only, not from every area; a key shift has no answer, so a copy moved the wrong way with lightness kept and nothing merged may pass every check but the move; an area under 5 % is never read; without a pack of crops its tile greys; the How's example is generated, its last frame grey.
- Demonstrations and the harness: each example the same every time; a colour kind's wheel unusable while How shows; the harness gives every lesson the same seed, so lessons on one sheet can repeat a task.
- Canvas beyond Candidates: a transform open when the process is killed is lost; QuickShape's polylines at most 6 sides; under about 700 points tall the layer footer runs off, under 750 wide the islands and a notice meet, under 500 tall the side bar runs off; inserting holds the file in memory (up to 50 megapixels); the third side button tested by injection only; whether the panel shows 10 bits is not reported.

## Working features

- Taper and tilt (S44, D71, schema 19): the brush panel lists by job Pencil, Ink Pen, Inker, Monoline, Hard Round, Soft Brush (each row's picture painted by the evaluator). Inker: the Ink Pen's pressure width narrowing at both ends to a tenth of its radius, over 2 diameters of its size at the start and 3 at the end (smoothstep along the path); while the pen is down the line grows from its point and its end under the pen is blunt; the frame after the lift narrows the end, and that is the stroke stored (undo, redo and reopen give it exactly). A dot (one sample) is never tapered; a stroke shorter than both tapers stays thinner than full width all along; the eraser tapers too, a smudge only at its start; the pen's outline shows the brush untapered. Pencil: unchanged up to 60 degrees of tilt (how a pen is held: the author's leans 45 at the median), then wider and lighter, at 75 degrees three times as wide with a quarter of the graphite. Monoline: one width at any pressure, full strength, StreamLine 60 % at first (every other brush 0 %). Hard Round: size 0.25 + 0.75 p and opacity 0.1 + 0.9 p of the stroke's, a hard covering edge. Ink Pen and Soft Brush draw as before; every stroke drawn before keeps its look (its recipe snapshot gains flat tilt curves and no taper). Brush files may hold `tilt` curves in `[size]` and `[opacity]` (degrees 0 to 90; a size up to 4 times), a `[taper]` (start, end, tip) and a top-level `streamline`.
- QuickShape (S42, D69, no format change): a stroke (brush, eraser or smudge) whose pen rests within 5 points for 0.5 s (by the input clock; frames keep coming while the pen is down) snaps to the shape it resembles: a line from where it began to where the pen rests, a circle's arc, an ellipse (a circle when its short axis is at least 0.9 of its long one), or a polyline of 2 to 6 straight sides with sharp corners (open, or closed as a triangle, rectangle, polygon); a stroke resembling none, or under 30 points long, stays as drawn (one try per rest). Still pressing, moving the pen turns and sizes the shape about its start (open) or middle (closed). The stored stroke is samples along the exact shape (at most 16 px and 5 degrees apart, and 0.5 and 2 px either side of each corner, so the curve through them keeps corners sharp), each carrying the pressure, tilt and time the drawn stroke had at the same share of its length (the azimuth of the nearer sample). After the lift a pill "Edit shape" in the top band's middle; tapped, the handles show (a line's ends, an arc's ends and middle, an ellipse's four axis ends: the axis turns to the hand, the other keeps its length; a polyline's corners; pen or finger, the Transform tool's marks) and the pill says Done; every drag replaces the stroke (and its copies) in the same history step, so one undo goes back to before the stroke; Done or any other intent but navigation and saving ends it. Recognition (`quickshape_fit.rs`): the path at 96 even points, the resting samples dropped; closed when its end comes within 0.2 of the size (bounding diagonal) of its start, cut where nearest, enclosing at least 0.03 size²; a line when no point strays 0.05 size from the chord and the path is at most 1.15 chords; else the closer fit (root mean square) of arc (Kasa least squares, turning 25 to 330 degrees one way) or ellipse (the enclosed area's moments) and polyline (Douglas-Peucker at 0.035 size, corners turning at least 30 degrees, sides at least 0.08 size, each side the least-squares line through its middle 70 %, corners where sides meet, the path passing within 0.03 size of each), a fit counting within 0.025 size on average and 0.08 at worst.
- Drawing Guide (S42, D69): Actions > Canvas ends with a Drawing Guide switch and Vertical, Horizontal, Quadrant, Radial as words in two columns (the one in use in the ink pill while on; a word tapped turns it on with that symmetry), and while on the hint "A finger moves the centre."; over the canvas faint lines through the centre (upright, level, both, or four 45 degrees apart) and a faint handle a finger drags (the pen never: a stroke begun at the centre draws). Every stroke (brush, eraser, smudge, QuickShape too) is drawn again mirrored (Procreate's default, not rotational: 1, 1, 3 or 7 copies, the radial ones the square's eight symmetries), live while drawn (the canvas shows the artwork with the copies under the stroke), each copy a stroke record with the stroke's style and selection, committed with it as one undo step (copies first, the stroke last). Fills, selections, transforms and adjustments are not mirrored. The guide is session state: every artwork opens with it off, and it is not stored.
- Pen buttons (S41, S43, D70, schema 18): Actions ends with a Pen section, Pen buttons with what the side buttons do as its note ("Eraser · Eyedropper · Undo" at first); tapped, the panel shows its page in place (and again when Actions reopens, until Back or the panel closes): Back, "For the buttons your pen has.", Side button 1, 2 and 3 (from the tip outwards: Android's `BUTTON_STYLUS_PRIMARY`, `BUTTON_STYLUS_SECONDARY`, `BUTTON_TERTIARY`) each with Nothing, Eraser, Eyedropper, Undo, Redo, Switch tool as words in two columns (the one chosen in the ink pill, sliding), then Eraser end, "Erases with the eraser's brush." Eraser and Eyedropper act while held: from the pen's next contact (hovering or touching down with it pressed) the pen erases with the eraser's brush (the Eraser button carries the ring, the hover outline is the eraser's) or picks (the loupe, the colour on each lift, every contact while held); released, the tool chosen before is back (released on the glass: after the lift). Undo, Redo and Switch tool (eraser from any tool, brush from the eraser) act once per press, hovering or touching (on the glass: at the lift); pressed together they act from the tip outwards. The eraser end erases with the eraser's brush from the moment it comes into range until it lifts or leaves, whatever the buttons do. The first button holding a tool wins, then the second, then the third. Held tools are never stored; the chosen map and tool are, with the toolbox (a schema-17 toolbox opens with the third on Undo).
- Palette and hex (S41, D70): the colour panel's header holds a field showing the colour as #RRGGBB; tapped, its text is selected and the keyboard comes up; Enter (the keyboard's Done) with six hex digits (# optional, either case) makes that the colour (and a recent one); anything else shows "Type a colour as #RRGGBB." and keeps the colour; leaving the field shows the colour again. Under the recent colours a Palette row (+ and Edit) and up to 24 swatches in rows of 8: + keeps the current colour at the end (grey when it is there or the palette is full), a tap on a swatch paints with it, Edit (then Done) makes taps remove swatches ("Tap a colour to remove it."). One palette (Procreate keeps many). Stored with the toolbox.
- Copy and paste (S40, D67, schema 16): three fingers swiped down on the canvas (their middle at least 48 points down, within 30 degrees of vertical, by 0.8 s) or Actions > Copy & Paste ("Three fingers down") open a pill in the top band's middle (under the islands while the selection or transform bar shows): Cut, Copy, Copy All, Duplicate, Cut & Paste, Paste, pencil grey where there is nothing for them; a command or a tap on the canvas outside closes it; a three-finger tap still redoes, two fingers still navigate. They act on the selection, or on the whole active layer with none (Copy All: on what every visible layer shows, without the paper). Copy and Copy All hold records (the layer's strokes and edits as a nested record at full opacity by Normal through its mask; Copy All a record per clipping chain and faded group), the pixels outside a selection cleared, for the app's run only (not stored, not the system clipboard). Paste puts them on a new layer "Pasted layer" above the active one, framed by the Transform tool at once; moved or not, Transform (or any other edit) commits layer and place as one step, undo at the first step takes the paste back, a committed paste deselects. Cut clears the selection's pixels (or empties the layer's paint), one step; Duplicate and Cut & Paste are one step each. Paste is grey with nothing copied or a copy from a canvas of another size; a copy of nothing says "Nothing to paste: the copy holds no paint." with OK. Everything pasted and cut reopens as left.
- Merge Down (S39, D66, schema 15): the layer footer holds, after Add Mask, an arrow onto a line and the words Merge Down. Tapped, the chosen layer goes into the one below as a nested record (its strokes, edits, mask, opacity, mode and whether it clipped, nothing baked); the lower row stays, chosen, with its name, place and switches; the canvas does not change: a layer clipped to the one below merges bit for bit at any mode, opacity and masks; two layers of one mode (Normal, Multiply, Screen) merge with at most one 8-bit level in rare pixels, the lower layer's enabled mask (and its opacity below 100 %) moving into a record of its own, so the merged row then shows 100 % and no mask. Elsewhere Merge Down is pencil grey with why in quieter small text at its row's end: Nothing below (bottom of the stack or of a group), Hidden layer, Locked layer, Modes clash (different modes, or Overlay, without clipping), Below clips, Has clips, Too deep (8 levels). Strokes, fills, adjustments, filters, smudges and transforms after a merge act on the whole merged layer; a merged layer merges again; duplicate copies it with new ids; one undo step; reopens, exports at 1x/2x/4x, versions and OpenPaint files carry it; the row's picture shows everything. While the mask row is chosen the footer shows the mask's options (choose the layer's row to merge).
- Layer actions (S38, D66, no format change): each layer row starts with a picture of its layer alone (52 x 39 points at most, the canvas's shape) on a light checker where the layer is empty, a mask row with its grey (inverted as it shows), a group row with none. Pictures are derived, never stored: the layer evaluated alone at canvas size and reduced by box means on the GPU (thin lines shown stronger: each pixel's coverage raised to its cube root), made on a worker one at a time, the topmost out of date first, only while the panel is open; a picture changes after a stroke lifts or an edit is made, never while the pen moves; another artwork shows none of the last one's. The footer's last row: Rename and Group (Ungroup) as words, the duplicate and delete icons (the move up and down buttons are gone). Duplicate: a copy right above in the same group, the same name, mode, opacity, switches, mask and content (its strokes under new ids), chosen. Rename: a dialog grown from the word, headed Rename layer, the name selected in the field "Name", Cancel and Done (grey without a name); Done or Enter renames; Cancel, back or a tap outside keep it. Moving: a finger or pen held still 0.4 s on a row lifts it (paper and an ink outline over the rows, never above the list's top), it follows the hand and the list shows the layer where it would land, the other rows sliding; let go, it lands there; the places are move up's and move down's steps (into and out of groups at their edges, half a row apart where two share a row); the list does not scroll meanwhile; a drop into a closed group opens it. Duplicate, rename and each move are one undo step; everything reopens as left.
- Library (S36, D62, D63): each gallery card has three buttons right of its title: options (three dots), duplicate, delete. Options grows a panel from the card headed by the title: Save as OpenPaint file… (".openpaint"), then Versions (newest first, each dated "5 Oct 2026 13:02" in the device's time zone, seconds added when two share a minute; with none "Kept when you leave it after a change.") and Close. Save opens the system's save dialog with "<title>.openpaint" (Cancel: nothing; failure "Couldn't save the file."; success "Saved as an OpenPaint file." with OK). "+" adds, under "From a file", Open OpenPaint file…: the system's picker on every file; a foreign file "Couldn't open the file: it is not an OpenPaint file.", a damaged one "…: it is damaged.", a newer one "…: it needs a newer OpenPaint.", unreadable "Couldn't read the file."; chosen, a new card comes first with the file's title and picture (never replacing one) and opens to exactly the canvas saved (strokes, fills, smudges, masks, groups, clipping, modes, adjustments, blurs, transforms, inserted pictures; not the toolbox, history or versions). Leaving an artwork saved since it opened or was last kept (to the gallery or out of the app) keeps a version of it as left, at most 10 per artwork, the oldest dropped; a version restores as a new card "<title> (<date>)", the artwork and its versions untouched; duplicates start without versions; deleting an artwork deletes its versions (the dialog says so). The search field in the top band's middle filters the cards as typed by titles holding the text in any case ("No title holds “…”." when none); it is not kept. Versions are records of their own, each an OpenPaint file carrying its schema.
- Ten-bit display (S37, D64, no format change): on the tablet the canvas the screen shows (at and below about 1.4 screen pixels per canvas pixel) and the sharp-zoom tiers above it hold 10 bits per channel and the window presents Rgb10a2Unorm in sRGB, which the display processor composites as it is; soft brushes, low opacities, blending and the bilinear zoom show four times finer steps (each a ten-bit code within one code of the exact value). Picking, ColorDrop, thumbnails, transform lifts, exports (8-bit sRGB), files and every canvas hash are exactly as before (each code reads back as the 8-bit value an 8-bit canvas holds). A device without ten-bit compute writes keeps 8 bits throughout. Debug builds only: `cargo device-input gradient on` covers the whole screen with a grey ramp from sRGB 100 (left) to 120 (right), its upper half rounded to 8 bits (about 21 bands, 137 px wide), its lower half at 10 bits (about 80 bands, 36 px); `gradient off` ends it.
- Export at a scale (S29, D55): Actions > Share opens with the words 1×, 2×, 4× (1× in the ink pill at every start; the pill slides) and the file's size in quieter grey (2880 × 1716 at 1× on the tablet's screen-size canvas). Save PNG… and Share PNG… write at the chosen scale; at 4× Save JPEG… and Share JPEG… are pencil grey with "Up to 2×" and taps go nowhere. Ink and pencil strokes are evaluated anew at the file's resolution (a hard edge antialiased over one file pixel; the pencil's paper at its finer level, its tone kept), and so are fill edges (S30) and transformed content (S31); inserted pictures, transforms made before S31, smudges, masks and adjustments come from their canvas data (patches and smudges bilinear). The file is rendered in bands of 512 rows and pieces at most 4096 wide, the PNG streamed band by band, the sRGB profile embedded; at 1× it is the canvas pixel for pixel.
- Smooth stroke paths (S34, D60, no format change): every stroke (brush, eraser, smudge; Ink Pen, Pencil, Soft Brush; on paint and masks, inside selections, under transform records) follows one smooth curve through the pen's samples instead of straight pieces between them; the samples Android makes up once a frame on the straight line between or past real ones are no corners. Pressure (width, flow, the pencil's contact) changes evenly along it; the pencil's graphite and the soft brush build up evenly along a fast curve. Zoomed in (2x to 8x tiers) and in 2x/4x exports a fast turn is round, not faceted; at 1x most spans draw as one straight piece (1.27 pieces a span on the author's strokes). While the pen moves, the last bit behind the tip may bend a little as the next samples arrive; the stroke left at the lift is exactly what the frame after the lift shows (the last frame, but for an end taper, S44; a smudge's last two spans follow at the lift). Old strokes show as curves too; two-sample and straight strokes are unchanged.
- Sharp zoom (S32, D58, no format change): zoomed in past about 1.4 screen pixels per canvas pixel, the canvas sharpens within a moment of the fingers standing still (0.12 s): the screen shows the canvas evaluated anew at 2x, 4x or 8x (the power of two nearest the zoom), the middle of the screen first, the edges a few frames later (a new tier on the tablet 0.4 to 0.8 s); an ink edge is about one screen pixel wide at every zoom, upright or turned; while the fingers move, what showed before is enlarged. Panning keeps the tiles held and fills the new edge in a few frames; the in-progress stroke is sharp as it is drawn, and so are undo, redo and every other edit (a large change shows the canvas's own pixels for a few frames, then sharp). At or below about 1.4 the screen shows the canvas exactly as before; picking, ColorDrop, thumbnails, transform lifts and exports are unchanged.
- Fill edges (S30, D56, no format change): a fill's edge is derived from its stored mask whenever a scene first draws it: where its pixels step along a slant or a gentle curve the edge is the line they sample, antialiased over one output pixel at 1x (slanted edge pixels partly coloured, as the ink's are), 2x and 4x; straight runs keep exact pixel edges, corners and curves tighter than about 15 px keep the pixel edge cut at 45 degrees; the inside is exactly its colour; a fill reaching the canvas's edge keeps its corners. Beside ink lines no paper shows at any scale (the flood's edge ring lies under the line). Fills on paint, inside a selection and on masks alike; a transform's lift stays hard; selection clips of strokes stay hard. The same gesture, preview, undo and reopen as S15.
- Gaussian Blur and Sharpen (S33, D59): two more rows in Adjustments. Each opens the same panel with one slider (Radius for the blur, Amount for sharpening) at 0 %, Reset, Cancel and Apply; the canvas shows each value at once (the blur softens and spreads paint, alpha too, never darkening the paper beside it; sharpening crisps edges), and the S28 rules hold: Apply (or the wand, any other edit or tool, leaving) keeps it as one step, Cancel and undo drop it, nothing changed records nothing, a selection limits it with a hard edge (the blur reads the paint just outside), on a mask it filters the grey the mask shows, hidden and locked layers refuse. 100 % blur is a standard deviation of 32 canvas pixels (1 % about a third of a pixel); from 4 px (12.5 %) the blur runs on means of 2 x 2 canvas pixels, from 8 px (25 %) on 4 x 4, enlarged smoothly (S35, D61: within 3 levels of the exact Gaussian); sharpening adds up to twice the difference from a 1-pixel blur. Strokes painted later stay sharp; the result is evaluated at canvas resolution and enlarged smoothly in 2x and 4x exports and in the sharp-zoom tiers. Stored as an adjustment (value, selection, place), reopens as applied.
- Adjustments (S28, D59): the wand after Actions in the left island (Procreate's place) opens Adjustments: Hue, Saturation, Brightness; Curves; Gaussian Blur; Sharpen (S33, above). Chosen, a panel grows from the wand: Hue, Saturation and Brightness sliders at 50 % (no change; hue turns the HSV hue, saturation scales from the Rec. 601 grey, brightness goes to black below 50 % and white above), or Gamma / Red / Green / Blue over a curve square (pressing the curve adds a point and takes it, points move between their neighbours, the ends up and down; each channel's curve is a table of 256 8-bit entries read linearly between the two around a colour, S43, so a soft gradient through a steep curve steps by one level, not by the slope), then Reset, Cancel and Apply. Every value shows on the canvas at once (S35, D61: after a slider's first value the layer's pixels before the adjustment are kept as a float snapshot and each value evaluates only the adjustment near the layer's content, the same pixels the commit shows); the pen draws nothing meanwhile, fingers move the view. Apply (or the wand, any other edit or tool, leaving the app) keeps it as one undo step; Cancel and undo drop it; nothing changed records nothing. A selection limits it (and stays); on a mask it changes the grey the mask shows (inverted masks too); what is painted later is not adjusted. Hidden and locked layers: "Can't adjust: the layer is hidden." with OK. Stored as an operation (values, selection, place), reopens as applied.
- Insert an image (S26, D67, no format change): Actions opens with an Add section holding Insert image… ("As a new layer"; Procreate's Insert a photo), then Share and Canvas. Tapped, the panel closes and the system picker (the reference's, PNG and JPEG) opens. Cancel: nothing happens. A file that is not a picture, damaged, above 50 megapixels or 64 MiB: "Couldn't insert the image: …" with OK. Moving, scaling, turning and the bar's taps are the S20 transform's steps (one resample from the picture's pixels); undo at the first step removes the picture and its layer. No room for a layer (`MAX_LAYERS`) or a hidden group: a notice with OK. Leaving the app commits any open transform first, so neither an inserted picture nor a transform is lost.
- Colour (S27, D53): everything inside is sRGB. Commands (Gallery in the left island, Close, Invert, Deselect, Flip Horizontal, Flip Vertical, Rotate 45°, Reset, Invert and Clear on a mask, Group / Ungroup, Continue filling, Done, OK, Retry, Cancel, Delete, Rename's Done) are bare words in ink with the press wash; unavailable ones pencil grey. - Layer masks (S24, D51): the highlighted layer's footer holds Add Mask (a plus and the words in ink, nothing else on the row; grey once the layer has one; G3 follow-ups). Tapped, a "Layer Mask" row (a sheet with a round window, its own eye) appears under the layer one step right, outlined: strokes and fills now go to the mask, the layer's row loses its outline; the footer then reads "Black hides · white shows" (a grey hint) with Invert, Clear and Delete mask. On the mask the brush paints its colour's grey (black hides, white shows, grey a share), the eraser paints white (reveals), smudge moves the mask's grey, fill (ColorDrop) floods the mask's own grey; selections clip as on paint; Alpha Lock does not apply. Tapping the layer's row paints on the layer again; tapping the mask row, on the mask (no undo step either way). Invert (the row says Inverted), Clear (white again), the eye (Turn mask off: the row says Off, the whole layer shows, the layer's paint becomes the target and the mask row cannot be chosen until Turn mask on) and Delete mask are one undo step each. A base's mask hides its clipping layers with it; a clipping layer's mask hides only itself; masks apply before opacity, mode and the group's fade. Transform on a mask refuses with "A mask can't be transformed. Tap the layer to move its paint."; a whole-layer transform moves the paint only. Everything reopens as it was, the target included.
- Groups, Clipping Mask, blend modes (S23, D50): every layer row ends with its mode's letter (N, M, S, O) before its eye. Under the rows, for the highlighted layer: Opacity, Normal / Multiply / Screen / Overlay in two columns (the layer's own in the ink pill), Alpha Lock, Clipping Mask, move up, move down, Group (Ungroup in a group), delete; for a layer in a group, the group's name and its Group opacity slider below. Clipping Mask: the row slides one step right with an arrow bending down; the layer shows only on its base's paint, by its own mode and opacity, the base's opacity and mode carrying the chain; a hidden base hides it; a clip at the bottom of the stack or a group is a base. Group puts the active layer's chain, or a lone layer and the one under it, into "Group n"; the folder in the header adds a new group with an empty layer; a group's row (chevron, name, opacity, eye) sits above its layers, which sit one step right; tapping the row closes or opens it (not stored); its eye hides and its slider fades the whole group; groups pass through (a Multiply layer in a group darkens what is under the group). Move up and down step out of a group at its edge and into a group beside it; a new layer joins the active one's group; a group goes with its last layer. Each change is one undo step (a whole slider touch one step); everything reopens as it was; a layer in a hidden group refuses fill and transform with the hidden-layer notice.
- Smudge (S21, D46): Smudge (a fingertip over its trail) sits between Brush and Eraser in the right island; tapped, it takes the ink ring; tapped again, its panel headed Smudge lists the same brushes (Soft Brush at first) with its own StreamLine. The side bar's sliders read "Smudge size" and "Strength" (70 % at first, per brush). The pen picks up the active layer's colour under the tip and drags it along the path, transparency too, stronger with pressure; nothing outside its path changes; pressed, the tip's outline shows (as with the eraser). Inside a selection only the inside changes (colour from outside is carried in); under Alpha Lock colour moves and alpha stays. Undo and redo are exact; a reopened artwork shows every smudge pixel for pixel. The line follows StreamLine strictly (no catch-up at the lift).
- Transform (S20, D45): the arrow after Selection in the left island turns the Transform tool on (ink ring; Brush and Eraser lose theirs; the Selection tool goes): the active layer's painted pixels, or those inside the selection, are lifted once (one GPU readback, 57 ms for 2289 x 1257 px on the tablet) and framed by an ink line on a paper line with four corner handles and a turn handle on a stem above; the frame fades in. The pen inside the frame moves the content 1:1, a corner scales it evenly about the opposite corner, the turn handle turns it about the centre by the short angle; fingers starting inside the frame move, pinch and twist it, outside they move the view; a finger also takes a handle. A pill in the top band's middle offers Flip Horizontal, Flip Vertical, Rotate 45° and Reset (grey until something moved). While on, undo and redo walk its steps; tapping Transform again, choosing Brush, Eraser, the eyedropper, Selection or any other edit commits it as one history step (nothing moved: nothing recorded); a committed selection transform deselects. ## Known limits

- Adjustments (S28, S33): no Pencil mode, no colour balance, gradient map, noise, motion or perspective blur; a curve point cannot be removed (Reset clears the curve shown); curves are tables of 8-bit entries read linearly between them (within 1 level of the exact curve on random inputs, tested; the composite curve applies to each channel's rounded table entry); previews (S35, D61): a slider's first value evaluates the layer in full and takes its snapshot (130 ms on the test artwork); layers under and over the adjusted one are evaluated again wherever its content lies each value (a busy layer below costs every value); zoomed in past 1.4 (a view tier) each value evaluates the tier's tiles in full; the snapshot (16 bytes per pixel of the content's tiles, up to 79 MB) stays until the next edit; on a mask or under a transform record an unclipped adjustment still reaches the whole canvas and resumes from no snapshot; the open panel covers the side bar's lower half; on paint `inverted` is stored but has no meaning.
- Blur and sharpen (S33, S35): previews reach about 30 values a second on the test artwork at every radius, sharpen 33 (above the 60 Hz frame: the kernel about 9 to 13 ms and the evaluation of the tiles near the content 15 to 20 ms on the GPU, the evaluator costing about 2.5 µs a tile; D47, `fail`, ROADMAP Candidates); blurs from 4 px are within 3 levels of the exact Gaussian, not it; Alpha Lock is ignored (the blur spreads alpha); content a transform record brought in from beyond the canvas is not filtered (the material holds the canvas only); the result is 8 bits per channel at canvas resolution, enlarged bilinearly at 2x and 4x and in the zoom tiers (a 1-pixel sharpen halo enlarges with it); the arena holding filter results and smudge residuals is bounded by the device's storage binding limit (`Admission` beyond, not measured on the tablet); a preview's earlier values give their block back.
- Colour (S27): artworks are sRGB only (P3 is the author's decision below); PNG `gAMA`/`cHRM`/`cICP` without `iCCP` are not read, grey ICC profiles and CMYK JPEGs' profiles are taken as sRGB; a 16-bit PNG is reduced to 8 bits before its conversion; out-of-gamut colours clip per channel (hue can shift slightly for very saturated P3 colours); converting costs one extra pass over the picture on the transfer worker (not timed); the system picker opens in the last folder used (on the tablet the author's OneDrive), so device routines go through its menu to Downloads.

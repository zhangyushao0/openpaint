## Working features

- Drawing: only the pen draws (D18); every stroke (brush, smudge, eraser) follows one centripetal Catmull-Rom curve through the pen's samples (Android's made-up samples are no corners), pressure, tilt and speed shape size and opacity, StreamLine smooths the stored path (D71). Undo and redo (two- and three-finger taps, the side bar) walk versions; nothing is lost when the app is left mid-stroke (the stroke is committed as its lift would). The pen's outline shows while hovering (D41).
- Brushes (D74): ten built-ins in four fixed sets plus Recent (Sketching Pencil; Inking Ink Pen, Inker, Monoline; Painting Hard Round, Soft Brush, Flat, Opaque Paint; Texture Textured Round, Speckle), each a recipe of fixed stages evaluated per pixel along the path: softness, size and opacity over pressure, speed and tilt, grain, deposit (cover or build up, the Pencil's canvas-fixed sketch paper, D38), taper (start, end, tip), shape (disc, square, ellipse, chalk, grainy, splatter, rough square; spacing, scatter, rotation, jitter). A tap on the chosen brush's row opens its editor in place: a live preview strip, every engine setting in ranges the engine never refuses, Duplicate, Reset or Delete, the Set row; sets are reordered by holding a row. Library, sets and Recent are stored with the toolbox. A stroke keeps its recipe's snapshot.
- Smudge (D46): a third tool on the same recipes, dragging the active layer's colour (transparency too) along the path; Alpha Lock keeps alpha; inside a selection only the inside changes.
- Layers (D50, D51, D66): add, delete, duplicate, rename, hide, lock, Alpha Lock, opacity, Normal / Multiply / Screen / Overlay, Clipping Mask, one level of pass-through groups with their own opacity, a layer mask each (black hides, white shows; Invert, Clear, on/off), a picture of each layer alone, rows moved by holding them 0.4 s, Merge Down as a nested record that never changes the look (refused with the reason where it would).
- Selection and transform (D43, D57, D69): freehand and rectangle lassos that add up, Invert, Deselect; strokes, fills and adjustments stay inside. Transform frames the layer or the selection (move, scale by a corner, turn by the stem's handle, fingers pinch and twist, Flip, Rotate 45°, Reset; its own undo steps) and commits a record, so content stays sharp however often it is moved. Copy, Cut, Copy All, Duplicate, Cut & Paste and Paste (three fingers swiped down, or Actions) work on records (D67). QuickShape snaps a stroke held still 0.5 s to a line, arc, ellipse or polyline, with Edit shape's handles after the lift; the Drawing Guide mirrors strokes in four symmetries (session state).
- Adjustments (D59, D92): Hue, Saturation, Brightness; Curves (Gamma, Red, Green, Blue; tables read between entries, D70); Gaussian Blur; Sharpen. Each previews at once as it would be kept, Apply is one step, Cancel and undo drop it, a selection limits it, on a mask it changes the grey; hidden and locked layers refuse with a notice.
- Fill (D37, D56): the colour dragged from its button onto the canvas floods the active layer's (or mask's) connected region, the threshold set by holding and sliding, Continue filling after a drop; the edge is derived from the mask and antialiased at every scale.
- Colour (D53, D70): HSV wheel, eight recent colours, a palette of up to 24 swatches, hex entry; the eyedropper previews in a loupe and commits on the lift. Everything inside is sRGB; inserted and reference pictures with an RGB ICC profile are converted; exports embed the sRGB profile.
- Pen buttons (D70): the pen's three side buttons (from the tip outwards) each do Nothing, Eraser, Eyedropper, Undo, Redo or Switch tool (Eraser, Eyedropper, Undo at first); held tools act while held; the eraser end erases.
- View (D85): two fingers pan, pinch-zoom (0.25 to 8 of fit) and rotate; a quick pinch fits; zoomed past about 1.4 screen pixels per canvas pixel the canvas sharpens at 2x, 4x or 8x within a moment of the fingers standing still. The screen's canvas and tiers hold ten bits on the tablet (D64); the blit reads four texels inside a tier's tile as one hardware bilinear sample.
- Gallery and library (D62, D63): cards with options, duplicate and delete; search by title; New canvas (screen size, square, A4 portrait or landscape); Save as / Open an OpenPaint file (`.openpaint`, opened as a new card); up to ten kept versions per artwork, kept when it is left after a change and restored as a copy.
- Export and reference: Save or Share PNG at 1x, 2x or 4x and JPEG up to 2x, rendered in bands (D55); Insert image… puts a picture on a new layer framed by the Transform tool; a floating reference window (pan and pinch inside it, the eyedropper reads it).

## Known limits

- Practice packs and outlines: the composition pack is built but not pushed to the tablet, and no exercise or picker takes a composition yet (the app reads its kind); the entry tier is mostly Twemoji figures (1280-px renders, titled by file code), open-licence full-figure cartoon art being scarce; the builder needs the network on a cache miss; the official pack's outlines come from a 320-px matte, so thin parts below it (a fish's barbels, a probe) are left out, and its bytes are this machine's; on the device the derivation for the user's own pictures reads one silhouette (thin parts such as legs, wires and handles can be lost; a hole is kept only where the ground shows through); a picture of one's own cannot be renamed ("Picture n"), My pictures is written whole at each change, and a picture shows at most 1,024 px on its longest side; the options' thumbnails fill in at their first opening; an import reads the whole file (up to 512 MiB) into memory; a pack imported by `open-file` shows its notice at the app's next frame.
- Painting speed: while the GPU's clocks are low a small brush's frames come every other vsync (event to present p50 15 to 18 ms; 10 to 13 with the clocks up): no frame starts while the one before is unfinished, and the screen pass ends about 9.5 ms after a frame begins. An in-progress smudge draws its new residual tiles' material from its layer every frame, and the lift of a smudge or a clipped stroke builds the scene (13 to 59 ms). A stroke with mirrored copies takes their tiles' snapshots again every frame. Snapshots hold 2 MB a 256-px tile. Presentation reaches the glass about 3.4 vsyncs after the frame is queued.
- The interface's frames: the layer panel's first opening over a dense artwork waits for its pictures (each layer drawn whole on a worker; a smudge's or a filter's material in it is one GPU piece of up to about 45 ms: 77 % of vsyncs and a gap of 10 on the kinds test artwork); a panel's first frame lays out what it shows for the first time (the colour, brush and Actions panels 15 to 20 ms of UI pass, the brush editor 19 ms); a real tap's opening shows one frame of about 20 ms near 0.75 s (the app's own work, cause not found); while the compositor holds the frames before, `present` waits up to a vsync on the main thread (input queues meanwhile).

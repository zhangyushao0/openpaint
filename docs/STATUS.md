## Working features

- Layer masks (S24, D51): the highlighted layer's footer holds Mask ("White: shows all"; grey once the layer has one). Tapped, a "Layer Mask" row (a sheet with a round window, its own eye) appears under the layer one step right, outlined: strokes and fills now go to the mask, the layer's row loses its outline; the footer then reads "Black hides · white shows" with Invert, Clear and Delete mask. On the mask the brush paints its colour's grey (black hides, white shows, grey a share), the eraser paints white (reveals), smudge moves the mask's grey, fill (ColorDrop) floods the mask's own grey; selections clip as on paint; Alpha Lock does not apply. Tapping the layer's row paints on the layer again; tapping the mask row, on the mask (no undo step either way). Invert (the row says Inverted), Clear (white again), the eye (Turn mask off: the row says Off, the whole layer shows, the layer's paint becomes the target and the mask row cannot be chosen until Turn mask on) and Delete mask are one undo step each. A base's mask hides its clipping layers with it; a clipping layer's mask hides only itself; masks apply before opacity, mode and the group's fade. Transform on a mask refuses with "A mask can't be transformed. Tap the layer to move its paint."; a whole-layer transform moves the paint only. Everything reopens as it was, the target included.

- Groups, Clipping Mask, blend modes (S23, D50): every layer row ends with its mode's letter (N, M, S, O) before its eye. Under the rows, for the highlighted layer: Opacity, Normal / Multiply / Screen / Overlay in two columns (the layer's own outlined), Alpha Lock, Clipping Mask, move up, move down, Group (Ungroup in a group), delete; for a layer in a group, the group's name and its Group opacity slider below. Clipping Mask: the row slides one step right with an arrow bending down; the layer shows only on its base's paint, by its own mode and opacity, the base's opacity and mode carrying the chain; a hidden base hides it; a clip at the bottom of the stack or a group is a base. Group puts the active layer's chain, or a lone layer and the one under it, into "Group n"; the folder in the header adds a new group with an empty layer; a group's row (chevron, name, opacity, eye) sits above its layers, which sit one step right; tapping the row closes or opens it (not stored); its eye hides and its slider fades the whole group; groups pass through (a Multiply layer in a group darkens what is under the group). Move up and down step out of a group at its edge and into a group beside it; a new layer joins the active one's group; a group goes with its last layer. Each change is one undo step (a whole slider touch one step); everything reopens as it was; a layer in a hidden group refuses fill and transform with the hidden-layer notice.

- Smudge (S21, D46): Smudge (a fingertip over its trail) sits between Brush and Eraser in the right island; tapped, it takes the ink ring; tapped again, its panel headed Smudge lists the same brushes (Soft Brush at first) with its own StreamLine. The side bar's sliders read "Smudge size" and "Strength" (70 % at first, per brush). The pen picks up the active layer's colour under the tip and drags it along the path, transparency too, stronger with pressure; nothing outside its path changes; pressed, the tip's outline shows (as with the eraser). Inside a selection only the inside changes (colour from outside is carried in); under Alpha Lock colour moves and alpha stays. Undo and redo are exact; a reopened artwork shows every smudge pixel for pixel. The line follows StreamLine strictly (no catch-up at the lift).

- Transform (S20, D45): the arrow after Selection in the left island turns the Transform tool on (ink ring; Brush and Eraser lose theirs; the Selection tool goes): the active layer's painted pixels, or those inside the selection, are lifted once (one GPU readback, 57 ms for 2289 x 1257 px on the tablet) and framed by an ink line on a paper line with four corner handles and a turn handle on a stem above; the frame fades in. The pen inside the frame moves the content 1:1, a corner scales it evenly about the opposite corner, the turn handle turns it about the centre by the short angle; fingers starting inside the frame move, pinch and twist it, outside they move the view; a finger also takes a handle. A pill in the top band's middle offers Flip Horizontal, Flip Vertical, Rotate 45° and Reset (grey until something moved). While on, undo and redo walk its steps; tapping Transform again, choosing Brush, Eraser, the eyedropper, Selection or any other edit commits it as one history step (nothing moved: nothing recorded); a committed selection transform deselects. Hidden, locked or empty layers refuse with a notice and OK. What the preview shows is the committed artwork, pixel for pixel, and it reopens the same.

- Motion (S19, D44, the approved demo v3): one critically damped spring on real elapsed time. A panel grows out of its island button's ink ring, sharing one ink outline with the island (an SDF chrome pass) while it pours down, its content fading in near the end (about 0.4 s); it goes back into its button faster (0.25 s). Switching panels: the old one retracts into its own button at once (0.12 s), the tapped one grows from its button 0.05 s later and finishes last; rapid taps retarget, nothing queues. Pills (notices from their right end, the selection bar from the lasso, the fill bar from its middle), dialogs (from the tapped card, the veil darkening with them), the loupe (from the contact) and the reference window (from the Actions button) emerge and go back into where they came from. Opening an artwork, the canvas grows out of its card's picture (or the New canvas row); going to the gallery, it shrinks into its card. Layer rows slide to their new place on add, reorder and delete, a new row fading in. A quick pinch glides the view to fit; any canvas contact ends the glide. The ink ring, switches' knobs and swatch marks draw in or fade where they are. Taps always reach the final layout at once; a closing panel takes no contact; strokes, navigation, slider drags, the ColorDrop dot and the hover outline never move on their own. Frames come only while something moves.

- Selection (S18, D42, D43): the lasso button after Actions turns the Selection tool on (ink ring; Brush and Eraser lose theirs) and shows a pill in the top band's middle: Freehand and Rectangle (the one in use outlined in deeper ink), Close while a freehand path is open, Invert, Deselect (grey with nothing selected). The pen draws from the canvas or the desk: a rectangle by dragging; a freehand lasso by dragging or tapping points, closed by touching down or lifting within 14 pt of its first point (ringed once it can close), by Close or by choosing Brush, Eraser or the eyedropper. Shapes add up. The path shows as ink on a paper line; the selection as a black-or-white outline just outside it and still diagonal stripes over a light dim outside, drawn in the blit (no animation). Strokes (any brush, the eraser) and fills stay inside; a drop outside says "Drop inside the selection to fill." While the tool is on, undo and redo step through the selections; off it, through the drawing. The selection is not stored; clipped strokes stay clipped after deselect and reopen.
- Look (S17, D32, D40): paper-white line art. The white canvas shows on the whole screen and at fit lies on a light warm-grey desk with a soft shadow, under a 48-point top band. Nothing spans the width: a paper island outlined in ink at the top left holds Gallery, Actions (a spanner), Selection (a lasso) and Transform; one at the top right holds Brush, Smudge, Eraser, Layers and the colour button (Procreate's order); the side bar island floats over the left edge, centred: the size slider (small dot below, large dot above), the eyedropper, the opacity slider (faint disc below, solid disc above), undo and redo. The active state is a deeper ink ring (tool, open panel, eyedropper, colour) or an ink outline (chosen row); pressing shows a faint wash. Icons are self-drawn ink lines, 1.5 points, round joins. A finger draws nothing (D18). One panel (actions, colour, layers, brushes) at a time.
- Text (S17): the system's sans (Roboto on the tablet) and Chinese face (Noto Sans CJK), both at weight 400, handed over by the Kotlin host from `/system/etc/fonts.xml`; 18-point body, 22-point headings. Without a system face the built-in one shows Latin text and Chinese shows as boxes (logged).
- Notices (S17): a failed save or open, a refused fill, export progress and results and the reference image's state are a pill in the top band left of the right island; the fill bar (ColorDrop) and the view's zoom are pills in the band's middle.
- Pen outline (S17, D41): hovering over open canvas, the current brush's outline at full size (at least 2 points across), one 1-point line, black over light and white over dark per pixel. Pressed with the brush: no outline (the ink is the feedback). Pressed with the eraser: the outline at the radius the stroke has at the pen's pressure, on the stroke's end (`render::tip_radius`). Hidden over controls, with a finger down and while picking. Hovering over an icon button, a swatch or a side slider shows its name.
- Eyedropper (S12, D41): the side bar's button; a contact (pen or finger) on the canvas or the reference picture shows the loupe, 76 points up and right of it (below it near the top): the colour under it over the current colour, which stays until the lift. Over a control or off the picture nothing new is previewed and the loupe follows. The lift commits the last colour previewed (joins the recent ones, the tool before returns); a lift with nothing previewed keeps the eyedropper on.
- Reference window (S13, D41): no title bar; a tap on it (finger or pen) shows a slim bar (move grip, ×) and a corner resize mark, hidden after 3 s without a touch on the window or by a touch outside. Dragging the bar moves the whole window; the mark resizes it (240 x 180 points to 85 % of the screen). In the picture one finger pans past 8 points and two fingers pinch-zoom (1x to 8x) about their middle with the canvas's dead zones; the canvas never moves with them, and the pen never paints there. No +/- buttons.
- Side sliders (S17): while held, a bubble shows the value and the brush's circle at its real size (size) or a swatch at that opacity (opacity). Lists have no scroll bar at rest: the last row is cut and fades; a thin line shows only while scrolling.
- Brushes (S16, D38): the Pencil is draweave's graphite on canvas-fixed paper, darkening where it crosses itself up to its opacity; the Soft Brush builds up under a bell tip (so does the eraser on it); the Ink Pen covers evenly. StreamLine (S16, D39) under the brush panel's rows, 0 % at first, per brush and tool.
- Fill (S15, D36, D37): drag the colour button onto the canvas; lifting fills the active layer's connected region (threshold 60 % at first, hold still 0.4 s and slide to adjust); after a drop the fill bar offers "Continue filling". One undo step; artwork content; hidden or locked layers refuse with a notice.

## Known limits

- Masks (S24): one per layer, none on groups; no thumbnail on the mask row; the mask's grey is the current colour's luma (no grey-only colour panel), so black and white are chosen on the wheel or from recents; a mask does not move with a whole-layer transform and cannot be transformed itself; no apply-mask or merge; an off mask's row cannot be chosen (its tap does nothing until Turn mask on); turning a mask on, off or inverting it re-evaluates the whole canvas; the footer grew by the Mask row (8 rows, 10 in a group); the eraser reveals (white) where draweave's hid.

- Groups, clipping, modes (S23): groups are one level, have no mode of their own and are never isolated; a group cannot be moved, renamed, duplicated or deleted as a whole (move or delete its layers; Ungroup); rows cannot be dragged; Ungroup drops the group's opacity; collapsing is not stored (every group reopens open); only Normal, Multiply, Screen and Overlay; modes and clipping act on sRGB-encoded values; the panel's footer grew to 7 rows (9 in a group), so on windows under about 700 points tall the panel runs off the screen and the list shows fewer rows; the projection tries a group command each frame to offer Group.

- Smudge (S21): large sizes are slow (above about 340 px frame work passes 16.7 ms at p95; 600 px runs at about 25 to 35 Hz); every contact runs two dispatches over its (2r + 3)^2 nodes, about 16 contacts a frame at a fast pen, and the footprint is evaluated again over everything beneath. It drags only the active layer. A residual costs 20 bytes per pixel of its 32-pixel tiles on the GPU (a long 120 px smudge about 5 to 10 MB); finished ones stay in the target's arena until a compaction drops those not shown; above the device's storage binding limit (not measured on the tablet) evaluation fails with `Admission`. Reopening an artwork or exporting evaluates every smudge cold, in order. Radii are capped at 300 px. Alpha Lock carries colour at full strength into painted pixels (draweave's rule), so a locked smudge's edge is hard.

- Transform (S20): Uniform only (no Freeform, Distort, Warp, snapping, Fit to screen); bilinear resampling without mips, once per transform (strong shrinking aliases, repeated transforms soften); a frame around thin content puts its handles over its inside, so the pen grabs a corner rather than moving it; a full-layer patch costs the canvas's RGBA bytes in memory, history (64 MiB budget) and on the GPU; the lift reads the layer back on the main thread (57 ms for most of a 2880 x 1716 layer on the tablet); a transform still open when the app is left is not saved; the moved selection is deselected; Rotate 45° and flips jump (no motion on canvas content).

- Motion (S19): rows passing each other on a reorder overlap their text for a moment; a canvas left rotated shrinks into its card upright from its bounding box; a canvas that was never opened from a card (start, restart) appears without growing; panels and surfaces of a screen left vanish without motion; the switch from a panel to a tool button (Brush while the eraser is in use) leaves other panels open as before. On the tablet the reference window and notice pills were not exercised (they need the system's pickers). Frame work while idle is about 1 ms above the S18 build (see above).

- Layout: on windows under about 750 points wide (the right island grew by Smudge) the two top islands and a notice pill meet; under about 500 points tall the side bar runs off the bottom; nothing is mirrored for the left hand. The side bar covers about 130 px of the canvas's left edge at fit.
- Hover: egui's hover label fades in rather than appearing at once; the outline is drawn only in the canvas blit (under the islands it is hidden by them). Chinese typed on the tablet itself is unchecked.
- Eyedropper: a contact that starts on the canvas does not sample the reference when it crosses into the window, nor the reverse; near the screen's top the loupe drops below the contact and can cover the top-left island.
- Reference window: place, size and zoom are kept for the app's run only; its bar keeps frames coming while it shows (3 s).
- Fonts: one weight (400) everywhere; the harness's Segoe UI differs a little from the tablet's Roboto.
- Pencil: the paper repeats every ~228 x 114 canvas pixels; no tilt response; far zoomed out the bilinear screen may shimmer; at a low opacity a heavy pencil reaches the cap.
- StreamLine: the stored stroke is the smoothed path; no corner smoothing; pressure never smoothed.
- Selection: no Add/Remove modes, feather, Ellipse or Automatic; the edge is aliased (a bit mask); each clipped stroke stores its mask's runs again (a few bytes per row) and clip masks are not counted in history bytes; the bar's buttons shift when Close appears; dragging a drop from the colour button at the screen's top edge can pull the system bar (injected input).
- Fill: no fill reference layer, no gap closing; the threshold resets with each artwork session; a pencil's grain stops a low-threshold fill at its specks; a full-canvas flood takes about 145 ms on the tablet; every scene rebuild re-uploads all fill masks.
- View: not stored per artwork; no rotation snapping, no four-finger tap, no flip; no mipmaps.
- A held two- or three-finger shortcut or a held drop asks for frames continuously.
- Injected gestures: `input` has constant pressure; a self-targeting instrumentation needs `--no-restart`; `input motionevent` at the top edge can pull the system bar; a hover without its exit leaves the input dispatcher refusing the next hover enter.
- Export: the flattened canvas only, on white. Reference: one picture for the app's run.
- Brushes: three built-ins; no brush editing. Colour: HSV wheel and recents only. Alpha Lock captured per stroke and per fill.
- Gallery: duplicate and delete are card buttons, not swipe; order by last change only. Layer panel: reorder by buttons only.
- Palm rejection is basic; with injected input a touch landing mid-stroke cancels the pen's gesture and the stroke is dropped.
- Presentation dominates latency (D22). Blending is in sRGB-encoded values. A save rewrites the head every time.
- `leave` waits at most 2 s; a stroke still being drawn when the app is left is not saved.
- Dev machine (2026-10-03): with about 11 GB of commit charge free, parallel test builds twice failed in rustc ("memory allocation ... failed", then "required to be available in rlib format"); a rerun passed.
- GPU tests (2026-10-04): `render/tests/stroke` opened a device per test, 12 at once; 2 runs of 43 hung for good (one thread spinning, GPU idle). lldb on a hung one: every test left was inside `Core::headless` in NVIDIA's driver (`nvoglv64`), one in `vkCreateDevice` loading a DLL (waiting for the loader lock), the others on the driver's lock, while an exiting wgpu GL instance thread held the loader lock in the driver's `DllMain` waiting for that lock: a driver deadlock, not a GPU wait. Now each GPU test binary opens one device (`fixture::core`, runtime tests' `gpu::core` and `Runtime::start_on`); 190 runs, no hang, 0.85 s instead of 8. `tests/clear_readback.rs` (2 tests) and `adapter::chrome`'s unit test still open their own. Every GPU wait (readback, `wait_idle`) ends after 30 s with `RenderError::Timeout`; a deadlock in the driver itself is not bounded.
- GPU tests fail (not skip) without an adapter. Golden hashes verified on one desktop GPU; only canvas pixels are golden.
- Each evaluation bins every finished op on the CPU; Vulkan on the dev machine stalls ~1.5 ms every ~4,000 `write_buffer` calls. Each canvas target holds two extra float textures (about 40 MB at 2880 x 1716) and the core 2.8 MB of paper; the Chinese face adds about 32 MB.
- egui gets pointer events from the first contact of each batch only; multi-finger gestures (canvas and reference window) are read from the batches directly.
- Scenarios: strokes, palms and lifts stay window pixels and are not checked against controls (a stroke that starts on a panel on purpose, `s9-layers`, keeps its pixels); canvas places are window pixels, not canvas coordinates, so a change of the fit area moves them; slider, wheel and reference-window spots are pixel offsets from the control's middle (a resized control moves them); transform handles are no controls (pixels). Screenshots are never compared; the system's dialogs are played by devtools; walk-throughs before S17 still say "top bar", and S12's still says the colour button changes while picking.
- Device commands: `device-restore` writes members over the library and deletes nothing, so a file created after the backup stays and the comparison reports it ("not in the backup"); `device-check` waits a fixed 3 s after launch and keeps the app's own error lines (the Adreno driver logs 12 harmless Gralloc lines per start); `device-input` validates numbers only, not that a point lies on the screen.

## Working features

- Drawing (D18, D41, D71): only the pen draws; every stroke follows one Catmull-Rom curve through the pen's samples, pressure, tilt and speed shaping size and opacity, StreamLine smoothing the stored path; undo and redo (two- and three-finger taps, the side bar) walk versions; leaving mid-stroke commits the stroke; the pen's outline shows while hovering.
- Brushes (D74): eleven built-ins in four fixed sets plus Recent (Pencil; Ink Pen, Inker, Monoline; Hard Paint, Hard Round, Soft Brush, Textured Round (V大's four: blocks, transitions, the airbrush, texture), Flat, Opaque Paint; Speckle), Hard Paint (硬边颜料) a round hard edge at full opacity, pressure on its size only, the practice's hard tip from the same file; a toolbox kept before gets it first in Painting by the record's rules; the chosen brush's row tapped opens its editor in place (a live strip, Duplicate, Reset, Delete, its set); sets reorder by holding a row; all kept with the toolbox; a stroke keeps its recipe's snapshot.
- Layers and tools (D46, D50, D51, D66, D70): smudge and eraser on the same recipes; layers with opacity, four blend modes, Clipping Mask, Alpha Lock, lock, one level of pass-through groups, a mask each, a picture each, rows moved by holding 0.4 s, Merge Down as a nested record that never changes the look; the pen's three side buttons each Nothing, Eraser, Eyedropper, Undo, Redo or Switch tool.

## Known limits

Limits that a dated line in ROADMAP's Candidates already names, with its way out, are not repeated here.

- Packs: the composition pack of format 3 left in the tablet's Download folder (2026-10-30) is refused on import since S116b (the library holds its format-4 edition). The entry tier is mostly Twemoji; the builder needs the network on a cache miss (an ambientCG material's archive is 60 to 140 MB, only its colour map kept); `practice-pack search met` fails since the Met retired that search (2026-10-01); a 320-px matte misses thin parts; the user's own pictures' outline is one silhouette; pictures show at most 1,024 px; an import reads the whole file into memory.
- Practice scope: four stages and stage 5's key shifts (abstract analysis and structure rotation to come); no re-tests or trajectories; kept errors and counts (codes 42 to 58) summed nowhere; the practice pens' sizes fixed, V大's transition round and textured tip in no kind; only divisions, whole copies and value masses take a composition.
- Sheets: an exported sheet shows strokes and fills only (the originals are the screen's); the pen chosen, All tools and the overlay's mode not stored; after Next an earlier exercise cannot be undone; a stroke after undoing past the first Compare counts as the copy; a kind's How shows again after a restart until its result is stored; a sheet in portrait shows the pair smaller; the eyedropper over an earlier exercise still remakes it from its generator; a test session started with an explicit number shows its derived number.
- Frames: a picture removed from its pack leaves its frames' shape; an upright pair leaves its slot's outer part empty; the faded ground is made on the frame loop (untimed on the tablet).
- Reading the copy: a mark is the last Draw or Correct line within 0.1 of the frame; a solid's or object's piece goes to the nearest part within 40 px, so a box drawn with Draw reads as the answer; an ellipse under two thirds round shows as drawn; a shape's fit takes the copy's lines nearest the outline (a copy far off fits its best half); the fit counts an eraser's cut within its radius, pressure not; a colour's numbers take each colour as chosen, not as mixed; white or bare paper reads as bare; the eyedropper compares one pixel.
- Geometry: estimates always in square frames; the solids' vanishing points not shown; strokes within 7° and a tenth of the longer's length are one edge (on simulated free pages 3.5 % of a steady hand's directions said apart, S84's join 2.0 %; a shaky hand 34 %); a hooked end is an edge of its own; free boxes drawn touching are one; Construction not stored; the summary shows no answer; a 1.14.0 scale of six or seven cells halves unevenly.
- Divisions and value masses: with the view turned a line's round end may pass the frame by half a pen; a division has no answer; strokes join only end to end (35° at most), a line erased in part counts its pieces; a whole copy's division layer is found by its name; value masses' answers only for pack pictures, the greys fixed (Oklab L 0.35, 0.6, 0.85), a middle rim where light meets dark, masses under 1 % merged; a whole copy's blocks compared by eye and probe only.

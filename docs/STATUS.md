## Working features

- Drawing: only the pen draws (D18); every stroke (brush, smudge, eraser) follows one centripetal Catmull-Rom curve through the pen's samples (Android's made-up samples are no corners), pressure, tilt and speed shape size and opacity, StreamLine smooths the stored path (D71). Undo and redo (two- and three-finger taps, the side bar) walk versions; nothing is lost when the app is left mid-stroke (the stroke is committed as its lift would). The pen's outline shows while hovering (D41).
- Brushes (D74): ten built-ins in four fixed sets plus Recent (Sketching Pencil; Inking Ink Pen, Inker, Monoline; Painting Hard Round, Soft Brush, Flat, Opaque Paint; Texture Textured Round, Speckle), each a recipe of fixed stages evaluated per pixel along the path: softness, size and opacity over pressure, speed and tilt, grain, deposit (cover or build up, the Pencil's canvas-fixed sketch paper, D38), taper (start, end, tip), shape (disc, square, ellipse, chalk, grainy, splatter, rough square; spacing, scatter, rotation, jitter). A tap on the chosen brush's row opens its editor in place: a live preview strip, every engine setting in ranges the engine never refuses, Duplicate, Reset or Delete, the Set row; sets are reordered by holding a row. Library, sets and Recent are stored with the toolbox. A stroke keeps its recipe's snapshot.
- Smudge (D46): a third tool on the same recipes, dragging the active layer's colour (transparency too) along the path; Alpha Lock keeps alpha; inside a selection only the inside changes.
- Layers (D50, D51, D66): add, delete, duplicate, rename, hide, lock, Alpha Lock, opacity, Normal / Multiply / Screen / Overlay, Clipping Mask, one level of pass-through groups with their own opacity, a layer mask each (black hides, white shows; Invert, Clear, on/off), a picture of each layer alone, rows moved by holding them 0.4 s, Merge Down as a nested record that never changes the look (refused with the reason where it would).
- Selection and transform (D43, D57, D69): freehand and rectangle lassos that add up, Invert, Deselect; strokes, fills and adjustments stay inside. Transform frames the layer or the selection (move, scale by a corner, turn by the stem's handle, fingers pinch and twist, Flip, Rotate 45°, Reset; its own undo steps) and commits a record, so content stays sharp however often it is moved. Copy, Cut, Copy All, Duplicate, Cut & Paste and Paste (three fingers swiped down, or Actions) work on records (D67). QuickShape snaps a stroke held still 0.5 s to a line, arc, ellipse or polyline, with Edit shape's handles after the lift; the Drawing Guide mirrors strokes in four symmetries (session state).
- Adjustments (D59, D92): Hue, Saturation, Brightness; Curves (Gamma, Red, Green, Blue; tables read between entries, D70); Gaussian Blur; Sharpen. Each previews at once as it would be kept, Apply is one step, Cancel and undo drop it, a selection limits it, on a mask it changes the grey; hidden and locked layers refuse with a notice.
- Fill (D37, D56): the colour dragged from its button onto the canvas floods the active layer's (or mask's) connected region, the threshold set by holding and sliding, Continue filling after a drop; the edge is derived from the mask and antialiased at every scale.
- Colour (D53, D70): HSV wheel, eight recent colours, a palette of up to 24 swatches, hex entry; the eyedropper previews in a loupe and commits on the lift. Everything inside is sRGB; inserted and reference pictures with an RGB ICC profile are converted; exports embed the sRGB profile.
- Pen buttons (D70): the pen's three side buttons (from the tip outwards) each do Nothing, Eraser, Eyedropper, Undo, Redo or Switch tool (Eraser, Eyedropper, Undo at first); held tools act while held; the eraser end erases.
- View (D85): two fingers pan, pinch-zoom (0.25 to 8 of fit) and rotate; a quick pinch fits; zoomed past about 1.4 screen pixels per canvas pixel the canvas sharpens at 2x, 4x or 8x within a moment of the fingers standing still. The screen's canvas and tiers hold ten bits on the tablet (D64).
- Gallery and library (D62, D63): cards with options, duplicate and delete; search by title; New canvas (screen size, square, A4 portrait or landscape); Save as / Open an OpenPaint file (`.openpaint`, opened as a new card); up to ten kept versions per artwork, kept when it is left after a change and restored as a copy.
- Export and reference: Save or Share PNG at 1x, 2x or 4x and JPEG up to 2x, rendered in bands (D55); Insert image… puts a picture on a new layer framed by the Transform tool; a floating reference window (pan and pinch inside it, the eyedropper reads it).
- Look and motion (D40, D44, D97): paper-white line art, ink-outlined islands floating over the full-screen canvas, the system's sans; panels grow out of their buttons on one critically damped spring; the chosen word or icon carries the ink ring or pill; a frame every vsync while a hand is there or the interface moves.
- Practice (D98, D99, D102 to D104): the gallery's first card, Today, shows the stage (Stage 1 · Lines and colours, Stage 2 · Single shapes: the last session's), the sessions finished of about 15 and Start practice (Continue practice while one is open; once 15 are done it offers the next stage). Its options button (Practice options) opens the stages to start at once (the next one early, an earlier one to revisit), the colour copies (below), the practice packs kept (title: pictures, how many with an outline), Import practice pack… (the system picker; a file that is no pack, or a personal one, refused with a notice, nothing changed) and Add my picture… (the image picker: the picture goes into My pictures, a personal pack kept on the tablet only, never exported or licence-checked; its outline derived as the builder's and kept only if it passes the same checks; the notice says which). A session is a sheet generated from its seed, revealed a pair of frames at a time. Stage 1: from the second session on a warm-up of four exercises of the kinds compared before (the families met in turn), then 6 single lines, 4 groups of combined lines (each arrangement once) and 6 colours (four low in saturation), about 28 minutes (23 for the first). Stage 2: a warm-up of three from every kind met (at most one long, a shape, a combination or a scale in turn by the session's number; lines, groups and colours in turn), 4 single shapes (tiers by the session's number: entry, entry, simple, simple; medium from the third, hard from the sixth; the last one the user's own picture when there is one; pictures shown before wait while fresh ones remain), a colour combination, a value scale and a colour, about 27 to 29 minutes; without any pack and picture, no shapes. The bar in the band's middle (Warm-up 1 of 3, Shape 2 of 4, Colour combination 1 of 1; How, Compare, Next or Finish). A family's instructions show by themselves until it was compared or warmed up with (a shape's: the outline as a line drawing, the ends marked with Sketch, the big masses and negative shapes before detail, then Draw and Compare). The chrome holds only what the exercise uses: Gallery and All tools at the top left, and beside the empty frame at the right a line's or shape's pens (Sketch, a light pencil; Draw, the ink pen; Correct, blue, after Compare) with Undo and Redo, or a colour's wheel with Fill frame (a combination's and a scale's below); All tools brings the full interface back. A shape's picture lies in its frame, a CC BY item's credit in small print under it. The pen draws only in the empty frame, its own samples (no StreamLine, QuickShape or guide), clipped to it; the eyedropper reads an original only after Compare. Compare, as often as asked, lays a line's original or a group's lines over the copy thick and semi-transparent red, a shape's picture at 35 % with its outline in red; In place or Aligned (moved and evenly sized onto the copy as one shape, never turned, sketch lines left out; a shape's fit never pulled by lines inside the copy; every fit found on its own thread, the card saying Aligning… meanwhile) with no numbers; a picture without an outline lies faint in place only, Aligned greyed and the card saying why; or each colour inside the other's frame with both on the wheel, arrows from the pick to the original, ΔE and the difference in words; anything laid in the frame afterwards takes it away. The first Compare stores the result (a shape's record keeps which picture), every later one counts. Next glides to the next pair at the zoom kept; Finish shows the whole sheet with its pictures and credits, what was compared, the colour judgement's radar and the means of colours, combinations (areas' ΔE, weak contrasts made strong) and scales (off even, uneven, out of order); One more starts the stage's next session. Sheets are projects the gallery never lists; records and packs live beside the artworks.
- Colour work and copies (D104): a colour combination is a picture generated from its seed, 4 to 6 areas as bands, patches or blocks (in turn by session), soft or hard edged, related colours with one contrasting, half or more low in saturation, one weak and one strong contrast; its tools the wheel, Fill frame with Undo and Redo, the pens Big (Opaque Paint 64 px), Small (20) and Soft (Soft Brush 100) and the eyedropper (the copy may be picked, the original not before Compare). Compare reads the copy's pixels (Reading your copy… meanwhile): the original over the copy in place at half strength (the slider Original shown sets it), a chip per area (the original's colour over the copy's), the chosen area's pair on the wheel with arrows and words and its outline on both frames, and the weak contrasts the copy made strong counted and drawn red on its edges; an area left bare says so. After Compare the eyedropper on a point of either frame shows both colours there on the wheel and rings on both frames. A value scale is a row of 5 to 7 cells between a given dark and light (grey or of a hue, in turn), the ends shown in the original's frame as two halves; the pen paints one cell at a time (landing on an end is refused with a notice), Fill cell fills the cell in hand and moves on; Compare shows the even scale in the original's frame, a bar per cell under the row (the even step a red tick, an uneven cell's bar outlined red) and the card names the cells off, out of order or empty. A colour copy (色彩临摹, 大作业) is its own project from the practice options (New copy of a pack picture, one not copied before; New copy of my picture once there is one; each copy listed with Open): the picture and an empty frame of its shape, 1,600 px on its longer side, the full interface, the bar "Colour copy n · How · Compare" with no Next; the pen and fills stay in the empty frame, the original is not picked before Compare; Compare lays it in place as strongly as the slider sets, then the eyedropper compares a point. A copy counts in no session, stage, warm-up or summary, and the Today card ignores it.
- Languages (D100): the whole interface in English or Chinese (简体), chosen in Actions' Language row (English, 中文, each in its own words), switched at once and kept with the toolbox; until one is chosen, the system's (the tablet's locale at start). Every word comes from the UI's text tables; the practice's Chinese uses V大's terms (叠图对比, 原位, 对齐, 草稿笔, 修正笔, 更红/更黄, 更亮/更暗, 更纯/更灰, 色彩组合, 明度推移, 色彩临摹, 大作业, 弱对比); the names the app gives (图层 n, 组 n, 粘贴的图层, 未命名作品, the built-in brushes, "副本") show in the language while stored in English; names the user typed show as typed. Chinese is set in the system's Noto Sans CJK beside Roboto, at the same sizes; a card's Chinese paragraphs never start a line with a comma or colon. Controls keep their English names in both, so scenarios, benches and device chores work alike.
- Version: Actions ends with "OpenPaint 1.5.0 · format 1" ("· 格式 1" in Chinese); the APK is `dev.openpaint` 1.5.0+format1, version code 10500, debug-signed (D79). Storage format 1 (D95) for the library, OpenPaint files and kept versions; a library or file of another format is refused untouched, saying it is from another version.
- Rendering (D91 to D93): one render core, the GPU's rasterizer, draws every scene for every reader (the canvas, view tiers, exports, picks, pictures, brush rows); the stroke being drawn from snapshots per 256-px tile taken ahead; frames every vsync while a hand or the interface moves, waiting for the frame before only for a stroke and at rest (D97); background work at the GPU's pace while no hand is there (D96); a display cache shows a reopened artwork complete in its first frame; a lost GPU device is made again from app state with a notice (D96).
- Memory (D85, D93): the derived GPU caches keep within a quarter of the device's memory (2,831 MB of 11,323 on the tablet), the least recently used unit going first, and let go at once when the app is hidden (`onTrimMemory`).
- Storage robustness (D77): every write is one atomic fjall batch; a crash, an install or a full disk mid-save leaves the last whole save; a record that cannot be read fails only its artwork's opening.
- Tooling: the harness's scenarios (51, `cargo scenarios`; `"system_language": "chinese"` starts one as on a Chinese tablet; `"system": "test_pack"` answers a picker with a small generated pack), `cargo practice-pack build <list> [out] --sheet|search` (the official pack from `apps/harness/packs/shapes.toml`, every licence checked, each outline derived and drawn on the tier's contact sheet, red kept, blue listed `no_outline`), the device chores of AGENTS.md (backup and restore with the packs, check, input, clean, push (`--download` into Download itself), info, smoke, reset; `device-input practice-test start [stage|copy]|end`: a test practice session or colour copy beside the author's, its name kept in `artifacts/device/practice-test`, removed with its sheet, D102; `open-file <name>.pack` imports a pushed pack), test artworks (`cargo test-art pile|a4|a4-plain|kinds|session`) and the on-device benchmarks (`cargo device-input bench open-cold|open|zoom|pan|strokes|session|export|touch|undo|adjust|ui|practice[@fast]`; `practice` on the practice sheet open at a line: How, a line, Compare, Aligned and In place, Next, in a stage-2 session a shape's outline, Compare, Aligned and In place, a combination filled, compared, its area 2 and the slider dragged, then the inline wheel, Fill frame and a colour's Compare).

## Known limits

- Practice packs and outlines: the entry tier is mostly Twemoji figures (1280-px renders, titled by file code), open-licence full-figure cartoon art being scarce; the builder needs the network on a cache miss; 66 of the official 126 pictures have no outline (pale bodies on pale grounds, cast shadows and pools of light, grounds that are no plain colour, painted patterns the edge follows), so their Compare is the faint picture in place only; the derivation reads one silhouette (thin parts such as legs, wires and handles can be lost; a hole is kept only where the ground shows through); a picture of one's own cannot be removed or renamed, My pictures is written whole at each addition, and a picture shows at most 1,024 px on its longest side; an import reads the whole file (up to 512 MiB) into memory; a pack imported by `open-file` shows its notice at the app's next frame.
- Painting speed: while the GPU's clocks are low a small brush's frames come every other vsync (event to present p50 15 to 18 ms; 10 to 13 with the clocks up): no frame starts while the one before is unfinished, and the screen pass ends about 9.5 ms after a frame begins. An in-progress smudge draws its new residual tiles' material from its layer every frame, and the lift of a smudge or a clipped stroke builds the scene (13 to 59 ms). A stroke with mirrored copies takes their tiles' snapshots again every frame. Snapshots hold 2 MB a 256-px tile. Presentation reaches the glass about 3.4 vsyncs after the frame is queued.
- The interface's frames: the layer panel's first opening over a dense artwork waits for its pictures (each layer drawn whole on a worker; a smudge's or a filter's material in it is one GPU piece of up to about 45 ms: 77 % of vsyncs and a gap of 10 on the kinds test artwork); a panel's first frame lays out what it shows for the first time (the colour, brush and Actions panels 15 to 20 ms of UI pass, the brush editor 19 ms); a real tap's opening shows one frame of about 20 ms near 0.75 s (the app's own work, cause not found); while the compositor holds the frames before, `present` waits up to a vsync on the main thread (input queues meanwhile).
- Smudges: cost grows with the radius squared (120 px: p95 70 to 105 ms; large sizes run well under 60 Hz; radii capped at 300 px); a smudge reads only the active layer; reopening or exporting evaluates every smudge once; where the residuals needed together pass what the device binds (about 140 default smudges crossing on the tablet) a frame fails and strokes there re-derive them.
- Background work and budget: a hand's first frame waits for the piece the GPU runs and one more (18 to 24 ms over the pile at 8x); both the frame loop and the raster worker submit; exports and filter kernels are not paced; a later tier's atlas is made on the main thread as it starts (20 to 40 ms); a read of the whole (a pick, the gallery picture, a transform's lift) draws what waits at once on its thread. The budget counts the app's allocations, not the driver's; under a tight budget the snapshots taken ahead may be taken again right after the budget let them go, and one near a smudge whose residual was let go waits for it, frames wanted meanwhile (ROADMAP Candidates).
- Adjustment previews: each value redraws its whole reach, on the worker's tiles when that is more than a frame's change, so over a busy layer no value shows complete while a slider moves (the last 0.26 to 0.28 s after it on the kinds test artwork); a retune while the worker holds the scene builds it anew (32 to 43 ms). Blur from 4 px runs on reduced means (within 3 levels of the exact Gaussian); filters ignore Alpha Lock and content brought in from beyond the canvas; curves are 8-bit tables read between entries; no Pencil mode, colour balance, gradient map, noise, motion or perspective blur; a curve point cannot be removed.
- Records: more than 16 selection moves or merged records nested at one place refuse to draw (`MOVE_DEPTH`); a selection cut keeps the mask's hard pixel edge; inserted pictures stay their 1x pixels; the lift reads the layer back on the main thread (126 to 140 ms for a busy full layer); a transform still open when the process is killed is lost (leaving commits it); Uniform only (no Freeform, Distort, Warp, snapping).
- Layers: groups are one level, never isolated, have no mode and cannot be moved or deleted whole; only four blend modes, in sRGB-encoded values; one mask per layer, none on groups, not moved by a whole-layer transform; Merge Down refuses where the look would change (Modes clash and the rest, D66) and over-merges may move a pixel one 8-bit level; a dragged row does not scroll the list; on windows under about 700 points tall the layer footer runs off the screen.
- Copy and paste: the clipboard lives for the app's run, not the system's, and pastes only onto a canvas of the copy's size; Copy All composites a faded group as if isolated.
- Shapes and taper: stamps lie on each span's chords (up to 1/16 px off the curve), spaced by size not pressure; grain reads the canvas grid (blocky at 4x and 8x); at most 48 stamps per pixel per span (`MAX_STAMPS`, a recipe limit); the taper is straight between knots (fast strokes taper in steps); mirrored copies show their end taper while the original is drawn; a smudge's end is never tapered; no pressure or opacity taper.
- QuickShape and the guide: no second-finger constraint; lines, arcs, ellipses and polylines of up to 6 sides only; the guide, its centre and symmetry are not stored; no rotational symmetry, grids or perspective guides; mirrored strokes and handle moves rebuild the scene and draw the copies whole.
- Fill: no fill reference layer, no gap closing; the threshold resets per artwork session; a pencil's grain stops a low-threshold fill; a full-canvas flood takes about 145 ms on the tablet; fill edges are smoothed only along lines (corners and tight curves keep a 45-degree pixel edge).
- Selection: no Add/Remove modes, feather, Ellipse or Automatic; aliased edges; each clipped stroke stores its mask's runs again; not stored.
- Colour and pictures: artworks are sRGB only; PNG `gAMA`/`cHRM`/`cICP` without `iCCP` are not read; grey and CMYK profiles are taken as sRGB; 16-bit PNGs are reduced first; inserting takes the whole file in memory (up to 50 megapixels); one reference picture for the app's run.
- Export: the flattened canvas on white, 8-bit sRGB, 1x/2x/4x (JPEG up to 2x); the encoded file is held in memory and written once; PNG encoding is most of a 4x export.
- Gallery and library: buttons, not swipes; order by last change only; titles cut at about 12 characters on a card; a version costs the artwork's records and an uncompressed thumbnail (478 kB at screen size), 10 per artwork, kept on every leave after a change; the picker offers every file; no intent filter for `.openpaint`; a long gallery may not scroll by finger (egui's default `ScrollSource`).
- Brushes and pen: no brush import or export, no renamed or added sets, a set longer than the screen runs off the panel; the editor shows no paper values and removes no curve point; the third side button was exercised by injected states only; a pen sending buttons only as key events does nothing; the hex field opens the text keyboard; one palette.
- Ten-bit display: stored content stays 8-bit (patches, pictures, filter results); a code may lie one off the nearest at an 8-bit step's edge; whether the panel shows 10 bits is not reported.
- Interruptions: a platform cancel while the app stays in front drops the stroke being drawn; `leave` waits at most 2 s for the store; fjall preallocates 64 MiB of journal; palm rejection is basic (a touch landing mid-stroke cancels an injected pen gesture).
- Memory: the scene and history grow with the strokes (history within 64 MiB); beside the app the driver's memory grows about 0.1 MB a stroke on the tablet and is not explained; meminfo's PSS leaves most GPU memory out.
- Layout: under about 750 points wide the top islands and a notice pill meet; under about 500 points tall the side bar runs off the bottom; nothing is mirrored for the left hand; egui's hover label fades in.
- Development GPU and tests: GPU tests fail (not skip) without an adapter; each GPU test binary opens one device (a driver deadlock between devices on the RTX 5070, 2026-10-04); every GPU wait ends after 30 s with `RenderError::Timeout`; Vulkan on the dev machine stalls about 1.5 ms every 4,000 `write_buffer` calls. Scenarios keep strokes, palms and canvas places as window pixels; screenshots are never compared.
- Practice: two stages (no others, re-tests, drills or trajectories yet; stage 2 holds shapes, a combination, a scale and a colour of week 2's exercises); the warm-up holds four exercises (three in stage 2) whatever their kinds' times and draws only on compared kinds; one of several near lines alike in shape, drawn alone, may be aligned onto its neighbour; a shape's fit uses the copy's lines nearest the outline, so a copy far off in shape fits on its best half; the originals and pictures are drawn by the screen over the canvas, so an exported or shared sheet shows the strokes and fills only; a fill dragged onto a sheet (All tools) floods its whole 2,920 x 3,160 layer before the frame cuts it (Fill frame fills the frame's region at once); a colour's numbers take each fill's and stroke's colour as chosen, not as a transparent stroke mixes it; the aligned fit takes the copy as it is now (corrections with it) and an eraser's cut within its size's radius, pressure not counted; a stroke drawn after undoing past the first Compare counts as the copy (stroke ids return with an undo); the practice pens' size and colour are fixed (All tools for others); the pen chosen, All tools and the overlay's mode are not stored; a sheet opened in portrait leaves the tools and card their room at the right, so the pair shows smaller; the brush panel's StreamLine slider still shows on a sheet, where it does nothing; until a result of a kind is stored its instructions show again after a restart; the harness gives every session the same seed.
- Colour work and copies: Compare reads the copy's frame back on the main thread (400² px for a combination or scale, in the frame after it settles); dragging the slider Original shown draws 76 % of the vsyncs on the tablet (nothing on the canvas is redrawn; cause not found); an area painted pure white, or left as paper, reads as bare; the eyedropper's comparison reads one pixel; a scale is checked on the wheel's HSV value, not perceived lightness, so a hued scale even in value may not look even; Undo and Redo walk the sheet's history, not the exercise's; a combination's picture is laid when the sheet opens; a colour copy cannot be removed, renamed or have its picture chosen in the app (one not copied before is picked; `practice-test end` removes a test one), keeps no versions of its own, and shows its original at most 1,024 px on its longer side over a 1,600-px frame.
- Languages: two (English, Simplified Chinese); names the user typed are never translated, and a default name typed by hand ("Layer 5") shows translated too; only a card's paragraphs are line-broken the Chinese way (other text is one line); curly quotes come from Roboto; Chinese typed through the tablet's keyboard is unchecked; a restored version's title is noted in the language shown and stays so; the system's language is read at start only (a change recreates the activity).
- Device tools: `device-restore` writes members over the library and deletes only packs kept after the backup (other files made after it are listed); `practice-test` keeps its name on the dev machine (a library restored after `start` leaves a name `end` forgets, nothing removed), starts only from the gallery and gives a removed test session's id to the next session; `device-input` has no timeout and validates numbers only; finger injection was refused on some days (cause unknown); the benches run on the app's main thread (no input meanwhile) and at the toolbox's sizes; `bench ui` feeds its input inside the app and counts vsyncs on its own clock, so the system's touch boost never comes: the display wakes at each motion's start and its second frame waits about 12 ms in `present` (a real tap shows no such wait on the glass); `device-smoke` needs every card on one screen and changes the toolbox; `device-reset` names the first backup of the day it found, not necessarily the newest.

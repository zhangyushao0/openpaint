## Working features

- Export at a scale (S29, D55): Actions > Share opens with the words 1×, 2×, 4× (1× in the ink pill at every start; the pill slides) and the file's size in quieter grey (2880 × 1716 at 1× on the tablet's screen-size canvas). Save PNG… and Share PNG… write at the chosen scale; at 4× Save JPEG… and Share JPEG… are pencil grey with "Up to 2×" and taps go nowhere. Ink and pencil strokes are evaluated anew at the file's resolution (a hard edge antialiased over one file pixel; the pencil's paper at its finer level, its tone kept), and so are fill edges (S30) and transformed content (S31); inserted pictures, transforms made before S31, smudges, masks and adjustments come from their canvas data (patches and smudges bilinear). The file is rendered in bands of 512 rows and pieces at most 4096 wide, the PNG streamed band by band, the sRGB profile embedded; at 1× it is the canvas pixel for pixel.
- Smooth stroke paths (S34, D60, no format change): every stroke (brush, eraser, smudge; Ink Pen, Pencil, Soft Brush; on paint and masks, inside selections, under transform records) follows one smooth curve through the pen's samples instead of straight pieces between them; the samples Android makes up once a frame on the straight line between or past real ones are no corners. Pressure (width, flow, the pencil's contact) changes evenly along it; the pencil's graphite and the soft brush build up evenly along a fast curve. Zoomed in (2x to 8x tiers) and in 2x/4x exports a fast turn is round, not faceted; at 1x most spans draw as one straight piece (1.27 pieces a span on the author's strokes). While the pen moves, the last bit behind the tip may bend a little as the next samples arrive; the stroke left at the lift is exactly what the last frame showed (a smudge's last two spans follow at the lift). Old strokes show as curves too; two-sample and straight strokes are unchanged.
- Sharp zoom (S32, D58, no format change): zoomed in past about 1.4 screen pixels per canvas pixel, the canvas sharpens within a moment of the fingers standing still (0.12 s): the screen shows the canvas evaluated anew at 2x, 4x or 8x (the power of two nearest the zoom), the middle of the screen first, the edges a few frames later (a new tier on the tablet 0.4 to 0.8 s); an ink edge is about one screen pixel wide at every zoom, upright or turned; while the fingers move, what showed before is enlarged. Panning keeps the tiles held and fills the new edge in a few frames; the in-progress stroke is sharp as it is drawn, and so are undo, redo and every other edit (a large change shows the canvas's own pixels for a few frames, then sharp). At or below about 1.4 the screen shows the canvas exactly as before; picking, ColorDrop, thumbnails, transform lifts and exports are unchanged.
- Fill edges (S30, D56, no format change): a fill's edge is derived from its stored mask whenever a scene first draws it: where its pixels step along a slant or a gentle curve the edge is the line they sample, antialiased over one output pixel at 1x (slanted edge pixels partly coloured, as the ink's are), 2x and 4x; straight runs keep exact pixel edges, corners and curves tighter than about 15 px keep the pixel edge cut at 45 degrees; the inside is exactly its colour; a fill reaching the canvas's edge keeps its corners. Beside ink lines no paper shows at any scale (the flood's edge ring lies under the line). Fills on paint, inside a selection and on masks alike; a transform's lift stays hard; selection clips of strokes stay hard. The same gesture, preview, undo and reopen as S15.
- Gaussian Blur and Sharpen (S33, D59): two more rows in Adjustments. Each opens the same panel with one slider (Radius for the blur, Amount for sharpening) at 0 %, Reset, Cancel and Apply; the canvas shows each value at once (the blur softens and spreads paint, alpha too, never darkening the paper beside it; sharpening crisps edges), and the S28 rules hold: Apply (or the wand, any other edit or tool, leaving) keeps it as one step, Cancel and undo drop it, nothing changed records nothing, a selection limits it with a hard edge (the blur reads the paint just outside), on a mask it filters the grey the mask shows, hidden and locked layers refuse. 100 % blur is a standard deviation of 32 canvas pixels (1 % about a third of a pixel); sharpening adds up to twice the difference from a 1-pixel blur. Strokes painted later stay sharp; the result is evaluated at canvas resolution and enlarged smoothly in 2x and 4x exports and in the sharp-zoom tiers. Stored as an adjustment (value, selection, place), reopens as applied.
- Adjustments (S28, D59): the wand after Actions in the left island (Procreate's place) opens Adjustments: Hue, Saturation, Brightness; Curves; Gaussian Blur; Sharpen (S33, above). Chosen, a panel grows from the wand: Hue, Saturation and Brightness sliders at 50 % (no change; hue turns the HSV hue, saturation scales from the Rec. 601 grey, brightness goes to black below 50 % and white above), or Gamma / Red / Green / Blue over a curve square (pressing the curve adds a point and takes it, points move between their neighbours, the ends up and down), then Reset, Cancel and Apply. Every value shows on the canvas at once; the pen draws nothing meanwhile, fingers move the view. Apply (or the wand, any other edit or tool, leaving the app) keeps it as one undo step; Cancel and undo drop it; nothing changed records nothing. A selection limits it (and stays); on a mask it changes the grey the mask shows (inverted masks too); what is painted later is not adjusted. Hidden and locked layers: "Can't adjust: the layer is hidden." with OK. Stored as an operation (values, selection, place), reopens as applied.
- Insert an image (S26, D52, no format change): Actions opens with an Add section holding Insert image… ("As a new layer"; Procreate's Insert a photo), then Share and Canvas. Tapped, the panel closes and the system picker (the reference's, PNG and JPEG) opens. Cancel: nothing happens. A file that is not a picture, damaged, above 50 megapixels or 64 MiB: "Couldn't insert the image: …" with OK. Chosen: the picture appears centred on a new layer "Inserted image" right above the active one (in its group), and the Transform tool is on at once (ring, frame, bar): a picture inside the canvas keeps its pixel size and shows pixel for pixel; a larger one is reduced once (triangle filter on premultiplied pixels) to fit inside, aspect kept; JPEG EXIF orientation is applied (a phone photo comes upright); PNG transparency stays; an embedded colour profile is honoured (S27). Moving, scaling, turning and the bar's taps are the S20 transform's steps (one resample from the picture's pixels); undo at the first step removes the picture and its layer. Tapping Transform or any other edit commits the layer, the picture and its place as one history step (even unmoved); one undo removes both, redo brings them back. No room for a layer (`MAX_LAYERS`) or a hidden group: a notice with OK. The picture's layer takes masks, clipping layers, blend modes and later transforms like painted content, exports as shown and reopens. Leaving the app commits any open transform first, so neither an inserted picture nor a transform is lost.
- Colour (S27, D53): everything inside is sRGB. A picture inserted or chosen as the reference with an RGB ICC profile (PNG `iCCP`, JPEG APP2; a phone's Display P3) comes in converted to sRGB: P3 (200, 100, 50) shows and picks as (215, 93, 31); colours sRGB cannot show are clipped per channel (P3's pure red becomes sRGB's); untagged and sRGB-tagged files (our own exports) keep every value; an unreadable, grey or CMYK profile: values taken as sRGB, a warning in the log. PNG and JPEG exports carry an embedded sRGB profile (612 bytes, the same in every file), pixels unchanged. The window surface is declared sRGB (`SurfaceColorSpace::Srgb`); the app logs what the surface offered at attach. Eyedropper and colour values unchanged (sRGB).
- Chosen marks and the press (G3 follow-ups): in a group of mutually exclusive choices in one container (the selection bar's Freehand / Rectangle, the layer footer's four modes in a 2 x 2 grid, the right island's Brush / Smudge / Eraser) the one mark slides and resizes to the item chosen with the settle spring (0.3 s; diagonally across the mode grid, narrowing or widening to the word); a new choice mid-slide turns it from where it is. It never crosses groups or islands: with Selection or Transform on, the tools' ring fades where it is and draws in at the tool chosen later; Layers' ring is its own. A group shown anew (a panel opening, the bar appearing, back from the gallery) has its mark in place; a group that moves (the footer under a new row) carries its mark at once. The press wash fills exactly the control's outline: the pill round a word (chosen or not), the ring's circle for icons and swatches, the row's outline for rows; hit areas stay 48 points and invisible.
- Text controls (S25, one ring rule): the ink ring means chosen, for words as for icons. Words that are one of a few are bare at rest; the chosen one sits in an ink pill (the icon ring's 1.5-point line and ink, as high as the ring, round the word, not the column). Commands (Gallery in the left island, Close, Invert, Deselect, Flip Horizontal, Flip Vertical, Rotate 45°, Reset, Invert and Clear on a mask, Group / Ungroup, Continue filling, Done, OK, Retry, Cancel, Delete, Rename's Done) are bare words in ink with the press wash; unavailable ones pencil grey. A faint upright line (outline weight) sets the selection bar's choice apart from its commands, and a notice's or the fill bar's message from its action. The mask's "Black hides · white shows" is a quieter grey hint. Hit targets and control names are unchanged (48 points); text fields keep their outline.
- Layer masks (S24, D51): the highlighted layer's footer holds Add Mask (a plus and the words in ink, nothing else on the row; grey once the layer has one; G3 follow-ups). Tapped, a "Layer Mask" row (a sheet with a round window, its own eye) appears under the layer one step right, outlined: strokes and fills now go to the mask, the layer's row loses its outline; the footer then reads "Black hides · white shows" (a grey hint) with Invert, Clear and Delete mask. On the mask the brush paints its colour's grey (black hides, white shows, grey a share), the eraser paints white (reveals), smudge moves the mask's grey, fill (ColorDrop) floods the mask's own grey; selections clip as on paint; Alpha Lock does not apply. Tapping the layer's row paints on the layer again; tapping the mask row, on the mask (no undo step either way). Invert (the row says Inverted), Clear (white again), the eye (Turn mask off: the row says Off, the whole layer shows, the layer's paint becomes the target and the mask row cannot be chosen until Turn mask on) and Delete mask are one undo step each. A base's mask hides its clipping layers with it; a clipping layer's mask hides only itself; masks apply before opacity, mode and the group's fade. Transform on a mask refuses with "A mask can't be transformed. Tap the layer to move its paint."; a whole-layer transform moves the paint only. Everything reopens as it was, the target included.
- Groups, Clipping Mask, blend modes (S23, D50): every layer row ends with its mode's letter (N, M, S, O) before its eye. Under the rows, for the highlighted layer: Opacity, Normal / Multiply / Screen / Overlay in two columns (the layer's own in the ink pill), Alpha Lock, Clipping Mask, move up, move down, Group (Ungroup in a group), delete; for a layer in a group, the group's name and its Group opacity slider below. Clipping Mask: the row slides one step right with an arrow bending down; the layer shows only on its base's paint, by its own mode and opacity, the base's opacity and mode carrying the chain; a hidden base hides it; a clip at the bottom of the stack or a group is a base. Group puts the active layer's chain, or a lone layer and the one under it, into "Group n"; the folder in the header adds a new group with an empty layer; a group's row (chevron, name, opacity, eye) sits above its layers, which sit one step right; tapping the row closes or opens it (not stored); its eye hides and its slider fades the whole group; groups pass through (a Multiply layer in a group darkens what is under the group). Move up and down step out of a group at its edge and into a group beside it; a new layer joins the active one's group; a group goes with its last layer. Each change is one undo step (a whole slider touch one step); everything reopens as it was; a layer in a hidden group refuses fill and transform with the hidden-layer notice.
- Smudge (S21, D46): Smudge (a fingertip over its trail) sits between Brush and Eraser in the right island; tapped, it takes the ink ring; tapped again, its panel headed Smudge lists the same brushes (Soft Brush at first) with its own StreamLine. Inside a selection only the inside changes (colour from outside is carried in); under Alpha Lock colour moves and alpha stays. - Transform (S20, D45): the arrow after Selection in the left island turns the Transform tool on (ink ring; Brush and Eraser lose theirs; the Selection tool goes): the active layer's painted pixels, or those inside the selection, are lifted once (one GPU readback, 57 ms for 2289 x 1257 px on the tablet) and framed by an ink line on a paper line with four corner handles and a turn handle on a stem above; the frame fades in. The pen inside the frame moves the content 1:1, a corner scales it evenly about the opposite corner, the turn handle turns it about the centre by the short angle; fingers starting inside the frame move, pinch and twist it, outside they move the view; a finger also takes a handle. A pill in the top band's middle offers Flip Horizontal, Flip Vertical, Rotate 45° and Reset (grey until something moved). While on, undo and redo walk its steps; tapping Transform again, choosing Brush, Eraser, the eyedropper, Selection or any other edit commits it as one history step (nothing moved: nothing recorded); a committed selection transform deselects. Hidden, locked or empty layers refuse with a notice and OK. Since S31 (D57) a transform is stored as a record (what moved and where, no pixels): the content is drawn again where it moved, so a line scaled up three times keeps a one-pixel edge on the screen and in 2× and 4× exports, and transforming again never softens it; pencil grain, fill edges, clips and smudges move with it. While the hand drags, the frame's content is the lifted pixels (bilinear, as S20); when it lifts (and after every bar tap, undo or redo) it shows as committed, pixel for pixel, and it reopens the same. An inserted picture and transforms made before S31 stay pixels; a later transform of either is a record.

## Known limits

- Adjustments (S28, S33): no Pencil mode, no colour balance, gradient map, noise, motion or perspective blur; a curve point cannot be removed (Reset clears the curve shown); curves go through 8-bit tables, so a strong curve bands a smooth gradient (measured above); each slider value re-evaluates the canvas (an unclipped adjustment reaches every tile of its layer): a hue value costs the main thread about 25 to 30 ms on the tablet on a light screen-size artwork (about 10 ms planning on the CPU: every one of the 19,440 tiles binned again; then about 14 ms waiting for the GPU to evaluate every pixel), the same from S28 to S33 measured like-for-like (2026-10-05): S28's "8.33 ms" were present intervals with two frames per value, S33's 22 ms the pen probe's frame work once the value and its GPU wait fell into one frame; the open panel covers the side bar's lower half; on paint `inverted` is stored but has no meaning.- Blur and sharpen (S33): previews run at about 30 Hz near 10 % and 8 Hz at 100 % on a light artwork (GPU-bound kernel of up to 193 taps per pass; D47, measured, not chased); Alpha Lock is ignored (the blur spreads alpha); content a transform record brought in from beyond the canvas is not filtered (the material holds the canvas only); the result is 8 bits per channel at canvas resolution, enlarged bilinearly at 2x and 4x and in the zoom tiers (a 1-pixel sharpen halo enlarges with it); the arena holding filter results and smudge residuals is bounded by the device's storage binding limit (`Admission` beyond, not measured on the tablet); each new value allocates a block until the arena compacts to what the scene shows.
- Colour (S27): artworks are sRGB only (P3 is the author's decision below); PNG `gAMA`/`cHRM`/`cICP` without `iCCP` are not read, grey ICC profiles and CMYK JPEGs' profiles are taken as sRGB; a 16-bit PNG is reduced to 8 bits before its conversion; out-of-gamut colours clip per channel (hue can shift slightly for very saturated P3 colours); converting costs one extra pass over the picture on the transfer worker (not timed); the system picker opens in the last folder used (on the tablet the author's OneDrive), so device routines go through its menu to Downloads.
- Insert (S26): one picture per pick, PNG and JPEG only; a picture larger than the canvas was reduced once when inserted (later transforms are records over its pixels, sampled once through all of them); a picture the canvas's height (a portrait photo on a landscape canvas) puts the frame's turn handle under the top band's bar (pinch the view out to reach it, or use Rotate 45°); decoding takes the whole file in memory (up to 50 megapixels, about 200 MB); an inserted picture costs its RGBA bytes in history (64 MiB budget) like a transform's patch; the refusal for a layer in a hidden group reads "Can't transform: the layer is hidden.".
- Masks (S24): one per layer, none on groups; no thumbnail on the mask row; the mask's grey is the current colour's luma (no grey-only colour panel), so black and white are chosen on the wheel or from recents; a mask does not move with a whole-layer transform and cannot be transformed itself; no apply-mask or merge; an off mask's row cannot be chosen (its tap does nothing until Turn mask on); turning a mask on, off or inverting it re-evaluates the whole canvas; the footer grew by the Mask row (8 rows, 10 in a group); the eraser reveals (white) where draweave's hid.
- Groups, clipping, modes (S23): groups are one level, have no mode of their own and are never isolated; a group cannot be moved, renamed, duplicated or deleted as a whole (move or delete its layers; Ungroup); rows cannot be dragged; Ungroup drops the group's opacity; collapsing is not stored (every group reopens open); only Normal, Multiply, Screen and Overlay; modes and clipping act on sRGB-encoded values; the panel's footer grew to 7 rows (9 in a group), so on windows under about 700 points tall the panel runs off the screen and the list shows fewer rows; the projection tries a group command each frame to offer Group.
- Smudge (S21): large sizes are slow (above about 340 px frame work passes 16.7 ms at p95; 600 px runs at about 25 to 35 Hz); every contact runs two dispatches over its (2r + 3)^2 nodes, about 16 contacts a frame at a fast pen, and the footprint is evaluated again over everything beneath. It drags only the active layer. A residual costs 20 bytes per pixel of its 32-pixel tiles on the GPU (a long 120 px smudge about 5 to 10 MB); finished ones stay in the target's arena until a compaction drops those not shown; above the device's storage binding limit (not measured on the tablet) evaluation fails with `Admission`. Reopening an artwork or exporting evaluates every smudge cold, in order. Radii are capped at 300 px. Alpha Lock carries colour at full strength into painted pixels (draweave's rule), so a locked smudge's edge is hard.
- Transform (S20, S31): Uniform only (no Freeform, Distort, Warp, snapping, Fit to screen); while dragging, the content is the lifted pixels resampled bilinearly (soft when enlarged) and turns crisp only when the hand lifts; the settle after a drag re-evaluates where the content lies once (up to the whole canvas: 31 ms frame work for a busy full layer on the tablet); a selection cut through paint keeps the mask's hard pixel edge, enlarged with it; shrunk below half, fills and the pencil's grain alias a little more than strokes (strokes and fill edges follow the footprint; grain, clips, masks are read nearest); a selection mask that interleaves with what it moves (every other column, say) doubles the content listed per record, and more than 16 such moves stacked at one pixel refuse to evaluate; inserted pictures and older transforms stay bilinear patches; a frame around thin content puts its handles over its inside; the lift still reads the layer back on the main thread to frame the content (126 to 140 ms for a busy full layer on the tablet) and the draft holds those pixels until the commit; killing the process mid-transform (an install) loses it, leaving the app commits it (S26); the moved selection is deselected; Rotate 45° and flips jump.
- Motion (S19): rows passing each other on a reorder overlap their text for a moment; a canvas left rotated shrinks into its card upright from its bounding box; a canvas that was never opened from a card (start, restart) appears without growing; panels and surfaces of a screen left vanish without motion; the switch from a panel to a tool button (Brush while the eraser is in use) leaves other panels open as before. On the tablet the reference window and notice pills were not exercised (they need the system's pickers). Frame work while idle is about 1 ms above the S18 build (see above).
- Choice marks (G3 follow-ups): a chosen word's ink colour changes at once while its pill slides; in the Overlay mode a black stroke on Layer 1 over the white paper vanishes (Overlay over white is white, W3C), so the harness's overlay screenshots show an empty canvas. Text controls (S25): while the selection bar shows, a notice pill (for example a drop refused outside the selection) lies over the bar's right end on the tablet's width (they did before S25; the divider made the notice 24 points wider).
- Layout: on windows under about 750 points wide (the right island grew by Smudge) the two top islands and a notice pill meet; under about 500 points tall the side bar runs off the bottom; nothing is mirrored for the left hand. The side bar covers about 130 px of the canvas's left edge at fit.
- Hover: egui's hover label fades in rather than appearing at once; the outline is drawn only in the canvas blit (under the islands it is hidden by them). Chinese typed on the tablet itself is unchecked.
- Eyedropper: a contact that starts on the canvas does not sample the reference when it crosses into the window, nor the reverse; near the screen's top the loupe drops below the contact and can cover the top-left island.
- Reference window: place, size and zoom are kept for the app's run only; its bar keeps frames coming while it shows (3 s).
- Fonts: one weight (400) everywhere; the harness's Segoe UI differs a little from the tablet's Roboto.
- Pencil: the paper repeats every ~228 x 114 canvas pixels; no tilt response; far zoomed out the bilinear screen may shimmer; at a low opacity a heavy pencil reaches the cap.
- StreamLine: the stored stroke is the smoothed path; pressure never smoothed. Pen input in the author's library (s33 backup, 292 strokes, 100,014 samples): 0 to 5 ms apart, about 300 Hz reported plus Android's made-up samples (82 a second interpolated on the chord, fewer predicted past the last sample); fast strokes space samples 16 px (4 to 8 px/ms) to 37 px (above 8, up to 97).
- Curves (S34): one curve through the knots, so a corner drawn as a sharp turn between densely spaced samples rounds over about one sample spacing (synthetic scenario polylines: up to 166 levels at the apex; the curve keeps within 0.25 of a chord of it); a sample Android made up that fits neither rule (or a real one that happens to lie on the line with the previous pressure) is taken as it is; the last two spans of an in-progress stroke may bend as samples arrive, and a smudge trails them until the lift; a span is flattened by the shader per pixel (up to 128 pieces, a quick box test each), so pathological strokes (a random walk turning at every sample) cost the GPU about 2.8x at 4x (busy export 86 to 242 ms) and 8x tiers likewise; the pencil golden differs on DX12 in one pixel by one level (float contraction in the flattening); the harness `replay` stops at "a tap on undo or redo did not act" (its side-bar taps are pixels from before S17, already so on the S33 build).
- Selection: no Add/Remove modes, feather, Ellipse or Automatic; the edge is aliased (a bit mask); each clipped stroke stores its mask's runs again (a few bytes per row) and clip masks are not counted in history bytes; the bar's buttons shift when Close appears; dragging a drop from the colour button at the screen's top edge can pull the system bar (injected input).
- Fill: no fill reference layer, no gap closing; the threshold resets with each artwork session; a pencil's grain stops a low-threshold fill at its specks; a full-canvas flood takes about 145 ms on the tablet; every scene rebuild re-uploads all fill masks and their edge values.
- Fill edges (S30): only edges whose pixels lie along a line (within 0.45 px over 6 px) are smoothed: corners, curves tighter than about 15 px, noisy pencil-bound edges and dots keep the pixel edge cut at 45 degrees, so a small round fill shows small facets at 4x (under its ink line); a smoothed edge moves up to half a pixel from the mask's, so smooth convex fills lose about 0.3 % of their area; two fills meeting edge to edge without a line between them can show a faint seam at slanted edges (each antialiased on its own); the edge is derived on the thread that builds the scene, so each fill preview derives its edge on the main thread (about 0.9 µs per edge centre on the dev machine; a full-canvas fill with 61,000 px of edge 130 ms), and again for each export and each layer sampling; selection clips of strokes and adjustments keep hard edges.
- View: not stored per artwork; no rotation snapping, no four-finger tap, no flip; no mipmaps (far zoomed out). Sharp zoom (S32): between 1 and about 1.4 screen pixels per canvas pixel the canvas shows magnified bilinearly (soft by up to 1.4 pixels); a tier shown away from its own scale is resampled (WYSIWYG, not bit-equal); drawing at 8x with a large Ink Pen (about 40 px) reaches p95 17.5 to 21 ms frame work on the tablet (default size within the floor); while a new tier fills in (0.4 to 0.8 s on a busy artwork) frames come at about 60 Hz; a tier near 1.4x holds 64 MB, two together up to 90 MB (the one before is dropped past that); a large change (undo of a long stroke, opacity, masks) shows the canvas's pixels where it reached until those tiles fill again; a pause of 0.12 s mid-pinch starts a tier; in the harness the input steps' follow-up frames fill every tile, so screenshots never show a tier half filled.
- A held two- or three-finger shortcut or a held drop asks for frames continuously.
- Injected gestures: during the G3 follow-ups (2026-10-04) every injected finger (also `tap <name>`) was refused by the input dispatcher while pen and hover went through; since S27 fingers, pinches and `tap <name>` work (cause not known; S32 used them throughout); `input` has constant pressure; a self-targeting instrumentation needs `--no-restart`; `input motionevent` at the top edge can pull the system bar; a hover without its exit leaves the input dispatcher refusing the next hover enter.
- Export: the flattened canvas only, on white, 8-bit sRGB, at 1x, 2x or 4x (no 8x or custom size; the scale is not remembered across starts); JPEG up to 2x (its encoder takes the picture whole); the encoded file is held in memory and written once (a busy 4x PNG about 31 MB); PNG encoding is about 90 % of a 4x export; inserted pictures and transforms made before S31 are their 1x pixels enlarged, the pencil shows finer grain at 2x/4x; the size beside the scale counts pixels, not a print size or DPI. Reference: one picture for the app's run.
- Brushes: three built-ins; no brush editing. Colour: HSV wheel and recents only. Alpha Lock captured per stroke and per fill.
- Gallery: duplicate and delete are card buttons, not swipe; order by last change only. Layer panel: reorder by buttons only.
- Palm rejection is basic; with injected input a touch landing mid-stroke cancels the pen's gesture and the stroke is dropped.
- Presentation dominates latency (D22). Blending is in sRGB-encoded values; the surface is 8-bit sRGB although the panel offers Display P3, 10-bit and f16 swapchains (decision 14). A save rewrites the head every time.
- `leave` waits at most 2 s; a stroke still being drawn when the app is left is not saved.
- Dev machine (2026-10-03): with about 11 GB of commit charge free, parallel test builds twice failed in rustc ("memory allocation ... failed", then "required to be available in rlib format"); a rerun passed.
- GPU tests (2026-10-04): `render/tests/stroke` opened a device per test, 12 at once; 2 runs of 43 hung for good (one thread spinning, GPU idle). lldb on a hung one: every test left was inside `Core::headless` in NVIDIA's driver (`nvoglv64`), one in `vkCreateDevice` loading a DLL (waiting for the loader lock), the others on the driver's lock, while an exiting wgpu GL instance thread held the loader lock in the driver's `DllMain` waiting for that lock: a driver deadlock, not a GPU wait. Now each GPU test binary opens one device (`fixture::core`, runtime tests' `gpu::core` and `Runtime::start_on`); 190 runs, no hang, 0.85 s instead of 8. `tests/clear_readback.rs` (2 tests) and `adapter::chrome`'s unit test still open their own. Every GPU wait (readback, `wait_idle`) ends after 30 s with `RenderError::Timeout`; a deadlock in the driver itself is not bounded.
- GPU tests fail (not skip) without an adapter. Golden hashes verified on one desktop GPU; only canvas pixels are golden.
- Each evaluation bins every finished op on the CPU; Vulkan on the dev machine stalls ~1.5 ms every ~4,000 `write_buffer` calls. Each canvas target holds two extra float textures (about 40 MB at 2880 x 1716) and the core 2.8 MB of paper; the Chinese face adds about 32 MB.
- egui gets pointer events from the first contact of each batch only; multi-finger gestures (canvas and reference window) are read from the batches directly.
- Scenarios: strokes, palms and lifts stay window pixels and are not checked against controls (a stroke that starts on a panel on purpose, `s9-layers`, keeps its pixels); canvas places are window pixels, not canvas coordinates, so a change of the fit area moves them; slider, wheel and reference-window spots are pixel offsets from the control's middle (a resized control moves them); transform handles are no controls (pixels). Screenshots are never compared; the system's dialogs are played by devtools; walk-throughs before S17 still say "top bar", and S12's still says the colour button changes while picking.
- Device commands: `device-restore` first saves the library as found (`<name>-found-<time>.tar`, the time UTC), then writes members over the library and deletes nothing, so a file created after the backup stays and the comparison reports it ("not in the backup"); `device-info` stops a query after 30 s with `DeviceError::Timeout` (what made `dumpsys SurfaceFlinger --list` hang once is not known); `device-check` waits a fixed 3 s after launch and keeps the app's own error lines (the Adreno driver logs 12 harmless Gralloc lines per start); `device-input` validates numbers only, not that a point lies on the screen.

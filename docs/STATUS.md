## Working features

- Drawing (D18, D41, D71): only the pen draws; every stroke follows one Catmull-Rom curve through the pen's samples, pressure, tilt and speed shaping size and opacity, StreamLine smoothing the stored path; undo and redo (two- and three-finger taps, the side bar) walk versions; leaving mid-stroke commits the stroke; the pen's outline shows while hovering.
- Brushes (D74): eleven built-ins in four fixed sets plus Recent (Pencil; Ink Pen, Inker, Monoline; Hard Paint, Hard Round, Soft Brush, Textured Round (V大's four: blocks, transitions, the airbrush, texture), Flat, Opaque Paint; Speckle), Hard Paint (硬边颜料) a round hard edge at full opacity, pressure on its size only, the practice's hard tip from the same file; a toolbox kept before gets it first in Painting by the record's rules; the chosen brush's row tapped opens its editor in place (a live strip, Duplicate, Reset, Delete, its set); sets reorder by holding a row; all kept with the toolbox; a stroke keeps its recipe's snapshot.
- Layers and tools (D46, D50, D51, D66, D70): smudge and eraser on the same recipes; layers with opacity, four blend modes, Clipping Mask, Alpha Lock, lock, one level of pass-through groups, a mask each, a picture each, rows moved by holding 0.4 s, Merge Down as a nested record that never changes the look; the pen's three side buttons each Nothing, Eraser, Eyedropper, Undo, Redo or Switch tool.
- Records (D43, D57, D59, D67, D69): freehand and rectangle selections that add up; Transform (move, scale, turn, Flip, Rotate 45°) commits a record that stays sharp; Copy, Cut, Copy All, Duplicate, Cut & Paste, Paste (three fingers swiped down, or Actions); QuickShape after the pen rests 0.5 s, with Edit shape; the Drawing Guide's four symmetries; Hue/Saturation/Brightness, Curves, Gaussian Blur and Sharpen previewed as kept, Apply one step.
- Fill and colour (D37, D53, D56, D70): the colour dragged onto the canvas floods its region, the threshold by holding and sliding, Continue filling, edges antialiased at every scale; HSV wheel, eight recent colours, 24 swatches, hex; the eyedropper's loupe; sRGB inside, RGB profiles converted in, sRGB embedded out.
- View, studio, files (D55, D62, D64, D85): pan, pinch-zoom 0.25 to 8 of fit, rotate, a quick pinch fits, the canvas sharp at 2x, 4x or 8x once the fingers stand still, ten bits on the tablet's screen; the home's Studio: cards, search, New canvas (screen, square, A4); OpenPaint files (`.openpaint`, opened as a new card); ten kept versions an artwork, restored as a copy; PNG at 1x, 2x, 4x and JPEG up to 2x; Insert image…; a floating reference window the eyedropper reads; the canvas's way back is Home (首页).
- Look, motion, languages (D40, D44, D97, D100): paper-white line art, ink-outlined islands over the full-screen canvas; panels grow out of their buttons on one spring; a frame every vsync while a hand or the interface moves; English or Chinese (简体), chosen in Actions and kept with the toolbox (the system's locale until then), controls named by their English in both; the Chinese written Chinese-first (S102): V大's words in the practice (原图, 明度, 纯度, 色块, 勾线笔, 素材包), Procreate's for the painting tools but the author's 压感, 防抖, 色环, one Chinese term for each English one, a test refusing 13 retired terms (原色 for the original's colour among them).

## Working features

- Drawing: only the pen draws (D18); every stroke (brush, smudge, eraser) follows one centripetal Catmull-Rom curve through the pen's samples (Android's made-up samples are no corners), pressure, tilt and speed shape size and opacity, StreamLine smooths the stored path (D71). Undo and redo (two- and three-finger taps, the side bar) walk versions; nothing is lost when the app is left mid-stroke (the stroke is committed as its lift would). The pen's outline shows while hovering (D41).
- Brushes (D74): ten built-ins in four fixed sets plus Recent (Sketching Pencil; Inking Ink Pen, Inker, Monoline; Painting Hard Round, Soft Brush, Flat, Opaque Paint; Texture Textured Round, Speckle), each a recipe of fixed stages evaluated per pixel along the path: softness, size and opacity over pressure, speed and tilt, grain, deposit (cover or build up, the Pencil's canvas-fixed sketch paper, D38), taper (start, end, tip), shape (disc, square, ellipse, chalk, grainy, splatter, rough square; spacing, scatter, rotation, jitter). A tap on the chosen brush's row opens its editor in place: a live preview strip, every engine setting in ranges the engine never refuses, Duplicate, Reset or Delete, the Set row; sets are reordered by holding a row. Library, sets and Recent are stored with the toolbox. A stroke keeps its recipe's snapshot.
- Smudge (D46): a third tool on the same recipes, dragging the active layer's colour (transparency too) along the path; Alpha Lock keeps alpha; inside a selection only the inside changes.
- Layers (D50, D51, D66): add, delete, duplicate, rename, hide, lock, Alpha Lock, opacity, Normal / Multiply / Screen / Overlay, Clipping Mask, one level of pass-through groups with their own opacity, a layer mask each (black hides, white shows; Invert, Clear, on/off), a picture of each layer alone, rows moved by holding them 0.4 s, Merge Down as a nested record that never changes the look (refused with the reason where it would).
- Selection and transform (D43, D57, D69): freehand and rectangle lassos that add up, Invert, Deselect; strokes, fills and adjustments stay inside. Transform frames the layer or the selection (move, scale by a corner, turn by the stem's handle, fingers pinch and twist, Flip, Rotate 45°, Reset; its own undo steps) and commits a record, so content stays sharp however often it is moved. Copy, Cut, Copy All, Duplicate, Cut & Paste and Paste (three fingers swiped down, or Actions) work on records (D67). QuickShape snaps a stroke held still 0.5 s to a line, arc, ellipse or polyline, with Edit shape's handles after the lift; the Drawing Guide mirrors strokes in four symmetries (session state).
- Adjustments (D59, D92): Hue, Saturation, Brightness; Curves (Gamma, Red, Green, Blue; tables read between entries, D70); Gaussian Blur; Sharpen. Each previews at once as it would be kept, Apply is one step, Cancel and undo drop it, a selection limits it, on a mask it changes the grey; hidden and locked layers refuse with a notice.
- Fill (D37, D56): the colour dragged from its button onto the canvas floods the active layer's (or mask's) connected region, the threshold set by holding and sliding, Continue filling after a drop; the edge is derived from the mask and antialiased at every scale.
- Colour (D53, D70): HSV wheel, eight recent colours, a palette of up to 24 swatches, hex entry; the eyedropper previews in a loupe and commits on the lift. Everything inside is sRGB; inserted and reference pictures with an RGB ICC profile are converted; exports embed the sRGB profile.
- Pen buttons (D70): the pen's three side buttons (from the tip outwards) each do Nothing, Eraser, Eyedropper, Undo, Redo or Switch tool (Eraser, Eyedropper, Undo at first); held tools act while held; the eraser end erases.
- View (D85): two fingers pan, pinch-zoom (0.25 to 8 of fit) and rotate; a quick pinch fits; zoomed past about 1.4 screen pixels per canvas pixel the canvas sharpens at 2x, 4x or 8x within a moment of the fingers standing still. The screen's canvas and tiers hold ten bits on the tablet (D64).
- Gallery and library (D62, D63): cards with options, duplicate and delete; search by title; New canvas (screen size, square, A4 portrait or landscape); Save as / Open an OpenPaint file (`.openpaint`, opened as a new card); up to ten kept versions per artwork, kept when it is left after a change and restored as a copy.
- Export and reference: Save or Share PNG at 1x, 2x or 4x and JPEG up to 2x, rendered in bands (D55); Insert image… puts a picture on a new layer framed by the Transform tool; a floating reference window (pan and pinch inside it, the eyedropper reads it).
- Look and motion (D40, D44): paper-white line art, ink-outlined islands floating over the full-screen canvas, the system's sans; panels grow out of their buttons on one critically damped spring; the chosen word or icon carries the ink ring or pill.
- Version: Actions ends with "OpenPaint 1.0.0 · format 1"; the APK is `dev.openpaint` 1.0.0+format1, version code 10000, debug-signed (D79). Storage format 1 (D95) for the library, OpenPaint files and kept versions; a library or file of another format is refused untouched, saying it is from another version.
- Rendering (D91 to D93): one render core, the GPU's rasterizer, draws every scene for every reader (the canvas, view tiers, exports, picks, pictures, brush rows); the stroke being drawn from snapshots per 256-px tile taken ahead; background work at the GPU's pace while no hand is there (D96); a display cache shows a reopened artwork complete in its first frame; a lost GPU device is made again from app state with a notice (D96).
- Memory (D85, D93): the derived GPU caches keep within a quarter of the device's memory (2,831 MB of 11,323 on the tablet), the least recently used unit going first, and let go at once when the app is hidden (`onTrimMemory`).
- Storage robustness (D77): every write is one atomic fjall batch; a crash, an install or a full disk mid-save leaves the last whole save; a record that cannot be read fails only its artwork's opening.
- Tooling: the harness's scenarios (45, `cargo scenarios`), the device chores of AGENTS.md (backup, restore, check, input, clean, push, info, smoke, reset), test artworks (`cargo test-art pile|a4|a4-plain|kinds|session`) and the on-device benchmarks (`cargo device-input bench open-cold|open|zoom|pan|strokes|session|export|touch|undo|adjust[@fast]`).

## Known limits

- Painting speed: while the GPU's clocks are low a small brush's frames come every other vsync (event to present p50 15 to 18 ms; 10 to 13 with the clocks up): no frame starts while the one before is unfinished, and the screen pass ends about 9.5 ms after a frame begins. An in-progress smudge draws its new residual tiles' material from its layer every frame, and the lift of a smudge or a clipped stroke builds the scene (13 to 59 ms). A stroke with mirrored copies takes their tiles' snapshots again every frame. Snapshots hold 2 MB a 256-px tile. Presentation reaches the glass about 3.4 vsyncs after the frame is queued.
- Smudges: cost grows with the radius squared (120 px: p95 70 to 105 ms; large sizes run well under 60 Hz; radii capped at 300 px); a smudge reads only the active layer; reopening or exporting evaluates every smudge once; where the residuals needed together pass what the device binds (about 140 default smudges crossing on the tablet) a frame fails and strokes there re-derive them.
- Background work and budget: a hand's first frame waits for the piece the GPU runs and one more (18 to 24 ms over the pile at 8x); both the frame loop and the raster worker submit; exports and filter kernels are not paced; a later tier's atlas is made on the main thread as it starts (20 to 40 ms); a read of the whole (a pick, the gallery picture, a transform's lift) draws what waits at once on its thread. The budget counts the app's allocations, not the driver's; under a tight budget the snapshots taken ahead may be taken again right after the budget let them go, and one near a smudge whose residual was let go waits for it, frames wanted meanwhile (ROADMAP Candidates).
- Adjustment previews: each value redraws its whole reach, on the worker's tiles when that is more than a frame's change, so over a busy layer no value shows complete while a slider moves (the last 0.26 to 0.28 s after it on the kinds test artwork); a retune while the worker holds the scene builds it anew (32 to 43 ms). Blur from 4 px runs on reduced means (within 3 levels of the exact Gaussian); filters ignore Alpha Lock and content brought in from beyond the canvas; curves are 8-bit tables read between entries; no Pencil mode, colour balance, gradient map, noise, motion or perspective blur; a curve point cannot be removed.
- Records: more than 16 selection moves or merged records nested at one place refuse to draw (`MOVE_DEPTH`); a selection cut keeps the mask's hard pixel edge; inserted pictures stay their 1x pixels; the lift reads the layer back on the main thread (126 to 140 ms for a busy full layer); a transform still open when the process is killed is lost (leaving commits it); Uniform only (no Freeform, Distort, Warp, snapping).
- Layers: groups are one level, never isolated, have no mode and cannot be moved or deleted whole; only four blend modes, in sRGB-encoded values; one mask per layer, none on groups, not moved by a whole-layer transform; Merge Down refuses where the look would change (Modes clash and the rest, D66) and over-merges may move a pixel one 8-bit level; a dragged row does not scroll the list; on windows under about 700 points tall the layer footer runs off the screen.
- Copy and paste: the clipboard lives for the app's run, not the system's, and pastes only onto a canvas of the copy's size; Copy All composites a faded group as if isolated.
- Shapes and taper: stamps lie on each span's chords (up to 1/16 px off the curve), spaced by size not pressure; grain reads the canvas grid (blocky at 4x and 8x); at most 48 stamps per pixel per span (`MAX_STAMPS`, a recipe limit); the taper is straight between knots (fast strokes taper in steps); mirrored copies show their end taper while the original is drawn; a smudge's end is never tapered; no pressure or opacity taper.
- QuickShape and the guide: no second-finger constraint; lines, arcs, ellipses and polylines of up to 6 sides only; the guide, its centre and symmetry are not stored; no rotational symmetry, grids or perspective guides; mirrored strokes and handle moves rebuild the scene and draw the copies whole.
- Fill: no fill reference layer, no gap closing; the threshold resets per artwork session; a pencil's grain stops a low-threshold fill; a full-canvas flood takes about 145 ms on the tablet; fill edges are smoothed only along lines (corners and tight curves keep a 45-degree pixel edge).
- Selection: no Add/Remove modes, feather, Ellipse or Automatic; aliased edges; each clipped stroke stores its mask's runs again; not stored.
- Colour and pictures: artworks are sRGB only; PNG `gAMA`/`cHRM`/`cICP` without `iCCP` are not read; grey and CMYK profiles are taken as sRGB; 16-bit PNGs are reduced first; inserting takes the whole file in memory (up to 50 megapixels); one reference picture for the app's run.
- Export: the flattened canvas on white, 8-bit sRGB, 1x/2x/4x (JPEG up to 2x); the encoded file is held in memory and written once; PNG encoding is most of a 4x export.
- Gallery and library: buttons, not swipes; order by last change only; titles cut at about 12 characters on a card; a version costs the artwork's records and an uncompressed thumbnail (478 kB at screen size), 10 per artwork, kept on every leave after a change; the picker offers every file; no intent filter for `.openpaint`; a long gallery may not scroll by finger (egui's default `ScrollSource`).
- Brushes and pen: no brush import or export, no renamed or added sets, a set longer than the screen runs off the panel; the editor shows no paper values and removes no curve point; the third side button was exercised by injected states only; a pen sending buttons only as key events does nothing; the hex field opens the text keyboard; one palette.
- Ten-bit display: stored content stays 8-bit (patches, pictures, filter results); a code may lie one off the nearest at an 8-bit step's edge; whether the panel shows 10 bits is not reported.
- Interruptions: a platform cancel while the app stays in front drops the stroke being drawn; `leave` waits at most 2 s for the store; fjall preallocates 64 MiB of journal; palm rejection is basic (a touch landing mid-stroke cancels an injected pen gesture).
- Memory: the scene and history grow with the strokes (history within 64 MiB); beside the app the driver's memory grows about 0.1 MB a stroke on the tablet and is not explained; meminfo's PSS leaves most GPU memory out.
- Layout: under about 750 points wide the top islands and a notice pill meet; under about 500 points tall the side bar runs off the bottom; nothing is mirrored for the left hand; egui's hover label fades in.
- Development GPU and tests: GPU tests fail (not skip) without an adapter; each GPU test binary opens one device (a driver deadlock between devices on the RTX 5070, 2026-10-04); every GPU wait ends after 30 s with `RenderError::Timeout`; Vulkan on the dev machine stalls about 1.5 ms every 4,000 `write_buffer` calls. Scenarios keep strokes, palms and canvas places as window pixels; screenshots are never compared.
- Device tools: `device-restore` writes members over the library and deletes nothing (files made after the backup are listed); `device-input` has no timeout and validates numbers only; finger injection was refused on some days (cause unknown); the benches run on the app's main thread (no input meanwhile) and at the toolbox's sizes; `device-smoke` needs every card on one screen and changes the toolbox; `device-reset` names the first backup of the day it found, not necessarily the newest.

## Working features

- Brushes (D74): eleven built-ins in four fixed sets plus Recent (Pencil; Ink Pen, Inker, Monoline; Hard Paint, Hard Round, Soft Brush, Textured Round (V大's four: blocks, transitions, the airbrush, texture), Flat, Opaque Paint; Speckle), Hard Paint (硬边颜料) a round hard edge at full opacity, pressure on its size only, the practice's hard tip from the same file; a toolbox kept before gets it first in Painting by the record's rules; the chosen brush's row tapped opens its editor in place (a live strip, Duplicate, Reset, Delete, its set); sets reorder by holding a row; all kept with the toolbox; a stroke keeps its recipe's snapshot.
- Layers and tools (D46, D50, D51, D66, D70): smudge and eraser on the same recipes; layers with opacity, four blend modes, Clipping Mask, Alpha Lock, lock, one level of pass-through groups, a mask each, a picture each, rows moved by holding 0.4 s, Merge Down as a nested record that never changes the look; the pen's three side buttons each Nothing, Eraser, Eyedropper, Undo, Redo or Switch tool.

- Fill and colour (D37, D53, D56, D70): the colour dragged onto the canvas floods its region, the threshold by holding and sliding, Continue filling, edges antialiased at every scale; HSV wheel, eight recent colours, 24 swatches, hex; the eyedropper's loupe; sRGB inside, RGB profiles converted in, sRGB embedded out.
- View, gallery, files (D55, D62, D64, D85): pan, pinch-zoom 0.25 to 8 of fit, rotate, a quick pinch fits, the canvas sharp at 2x, 4x or 8x once the fingers stand still, ten bits on the tablet's screen; cards, search, New canvas (screen, square, A4); OpenPaint files (`.openpaint`, opened as a new card); ten kept versions an artwork, restored as a copy; PNG at 1x, 2x, 4x and JPEG up to 2x; Insert image…; a floating reference window the eyedropper reads.
- Look, motion, languages (D40, D44, D97, D100): paper-white line art, ink-outlined islands over the full-screen canvas; panels grow out of their buttons on one spring; a frame every vsync while a hand or the interface moves; English or Chinese (简体), chosen in Actions and kept with the toolbox (the system's locale until then), the practice in V大's terms, controls named by their English in both.

## Known limits

Limits that a dated line in ROADMAP's Candidates already names, with its way out, are not repeated here.

- Packs: the rebuilt composition pack (format 3) was left in the tablet's Download folder (2026-10-30); since S97's run the author's library holds two packs. The entry tier is mostly Twemoji; the builder needs the network on a cache miss; a 320-px matte misses thin parts; the user's own pictures' outline is one silhouette; pictures show at most 1,024 px; an import reads the whole file into memory.
- Practice scope: four stages; no re-tests, trajectories, key shifts or gamut masks; kept errors (codes 42 to 55) summed nowhere; the practice pens' sizes fixed, V大's transition round and textured tip in no kind; only divisions, whole copies and value masses take a composition.
- Sheets: an exported sheet shows strokes and fills only (the originals are the screen's); the pen chosen, All tools and the overlay's mode not stored; after Next an earlier exercise cannot be undone; a stroke after undoing past the first Compare counts as the copy; a kind's How shows again after a restart until its result is stored; a sheet in portrait shows the pair smaller.
- Frames: a picture no pack holds shows square frames; an upright pair leaves its slot's outer part empty; the faded ground is made on the frame loop (untimed on the tablet); the store's upgrade moves strokes and fills only, a move there and back rounding (9 pixels one level off in `s99-picture-frames`); a sheet laid anew without its pack keeps that copy as it lay.
- Reading the copy: a mark is the last Draw or Correct line within 0.1 of the frame; a solid's or object's piece goes to the nearest part within 40 px, so a box drawn with Draw reads as the answer; an ellipse under two thirds round shows as drawn; a shape's fit takes the copy's lines nearest the outline (a copy far off fits its best half); the fit counts an eraser's cut within its radius, pressure not; a colour's numbers take each colour as chosen, not as mixed; white or bare paper reads as bare; the eyedropper compares one pixel.
- Geometry: estimates always in square frames; the solids' vanishing points not shown; strokes within 7° and a tenth of the longer's length are one edge (on simulated free pages 3.5 % of a steady hand's directions said apart, S84's join 2.0 %; a shaky hand 34 %); a hooked end is an edge of its own; free boxes drawn touching are one; Construction not stored; the summary shows no answer; a 1.14.0 scale of six or seven cells halves unevenly.
- Divisions and value masses: with the view turned a line's round end may pass the frame by half a pen; a division has no answer; strokes join only end to end (35° at most), a line erased in part counts its pieces; a whole copy's division layer is found by its name; value masses' answers only for pack pictures, the greys fixed (Oklab L 0.35, 0.6, 0.85), a middle rim where light meets dark, masses under 1 % merged; a whole copy's blocks compared by eye and probe only.
- Colour table, copies, book: sessions before 1.6.1 count in the radar's last three axes only; bars scaled within a card; a How card fits in landscape only at four or five short paragraphs; a colour copy keeps no versions; book time before 1.7.0 only between stored times, a crash loses up to a minute, spans in UTC.
- Combinations (S101): a crop's areas are a squint at 128 cells: an area under 5 % of the crop or too thin for a core goes into its neighbour, so a small accent is painted but never read; a crop's recognisable objects are caught only by eye on the sheets; the official crops number 36 (most photographs tried shade one colour into another, most painting corners show objects), five of them torn posters in navy and grey; the How plays on a generated example; the scenarios' crops are generated pictures (`test_crops`).
- Demonstrations and drills: each example the same every time, its steps under it; a colour kind's wheel unusable while How shows; a drill's picture counts among sessions in My pictures' delete dialog; the harness gives every sheet one seed.
- Canvas beyond Candidates: a transform open when the process is killed is lost; QuickShape's polylines at most 6 sides; under about 700 points tall the layer footer runs off, under 750 wide the islands and a notice meet, under 500 tall the side bar runs off; inserting holds the file in memory (up to 50 megapixels); the third side button tested by injection only; whether the panel shows 10 bits is not reported.
- Interruptions and memory: `leave` waits at most 2 s for the store; fjall preallocates 64 MiB; the budget counts the app's allocations, not the driver's; PSS leaves most GPU memory out.
- Development: the gate and the scenarios each use every core, so anything heavy beside them (another test run, clippy) makes tests time out or the suite run out of memory (S104); a failed test's directory under `target/tmp` (a scenario's under `artifacts/scenarios/`) is kept and printed, a passing one's removed; GPU tests fail without an adapter, one device per binary (a driver deadlock on the RTX 5070); canvas hashes are exact per GPU driver (NVIDIA 621.01 re-took 20 scenarios' and two brush goldens' at S93).
- Device tools: `device-restore` deletes only packs kept after the backup; `practice-test` keeps its name on the dev machine and starts only from the gallery; `device-input` has no timeout; finger injection was refused on some days; `bench practice` measures How's first 2 s, no colour copy, no sheet's opening, and exits 0 with no sheet open; `device-smoke` needs every card on one screen; `device-screens` not_run on the tablet (no screenshot folder, 2026-10-28).

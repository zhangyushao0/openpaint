## Known limits

Limits that a dated line in ROADMAP's Candidates already names, with its way out, are not repeated here.

- Packs: the entry tier is mostly Twemoji; the builder needs the network on a cache miss; `practice-pack search met` fails (the Met retired that search); a 320-px matte misses thin parts; the user's own pictures' outline is one silhouette; pictures show at most 1,024 px; an import reads the whole file into memory (the combinations pack is 125 MB); sources: the Art Institute serves only `!3000,3000`, Openverse 200 requests a day (its Flickr pictures too small), the Smithsonian and Flickr need a key (unused), some large Commons scans fail; a photo added for a drawing from life but never drawn against is a shape lesson's picture.
- Practice scope: kept counts and shares (codes 30 to 34, 49 to 62) read by no model; a lenient eye earns dots as fast as the app's clean; the practice pens' sizes fixed, V大's transition round and textured tip in no kind; only divisions, whole copies, value masses, abstract analysis and contrast thumbnails take a composition.
- Progress (D124): a curve only from what was judged or paired; a pair of any two compared copies, judged or not (S150). - The path: some levels are weak: divisions, whole copies and value masses fall back to medium where a pack holds no hard composition. The review row carries no count.
- The Pomodoro: a test chore's block under way across a restart ends showing the setting's length; a lesson the chain left on another sheet counts as begun, so does one drawn before the block; a How's words checked by reading and by words only the app's workings use (S146).
